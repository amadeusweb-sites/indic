# Wars of the Wainriders

*Gondor's eastern wars · 1851–1944 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Wainrider wars were a series of invasions and counterattacks between Gondor and the peoples east of the Anduin. The Wainriders' first major victory at the Battle of the Plains in 1856 cost Gondor the life of King Narmacil II and much of its influence in Rhovanion. Gondor later checked their advance, but the Wainriders returned in 1944 alongside the Haradrim. King Ondoher and his sons fell in the northern fighting before Eärnil destroyed the main Wainrider force at its camp.

## Causes and Context

The Wainriders were part of the movement of peoples from the East that threatened Gondor's control of Rhovanion and its alliances with the Northmen. Their attacks exposed the difficulty of defending lands beyond the Anduin, where Gondor depended on distance and allied peoples to maintain its influence.

The conflict unfolded in waves rather than as one continuous campaign. Gondor's defeat in 1856 was followed by a period of resistance and recovery. The Wainriders later coordinated with the Haradrim, creating a simultaneous threat on Gondor's northern and southern fronts.

---

## Course of the Wars

1. **The Wainriders enter Rhovanion.** Beginning around 1851, the eastern people pressed into the lands beyond the Anduin and threatened Gondor's Northman allies.

2. **Gondor loses the Battle of the Plains.** In 1856, King Narmacil II led Gondor's army into battle and was killed. The Wainriders gained control over much of Rhovanion, weakening Gondor's eastern defenses. This engagement is covered in the [Battle of the Plains](../battles/plains.md).

3. **Gondor checks their advance.** King Calimehtar later defeated the Wainriders at Dagorlad in 1899. The victory gave Gondor a period of relief, but did not remove the Wainriders as a threat. This earlier engagement is distinct from the Last Alliance's much later Battle of Dagorlad.

4. **The invasion returns in 1944.** The Wainriders joined with the Haradrim and attacked Gondor on two fronts. King Ondoher and his sons were killed in the northern fighting, leaving Gondor in crisis.

5. **Eärnil destroys the main force.** Eärnil first defeated the Southrons in the south, then marched north and surprised the Wainriders at their camp. The [Battle of the Camp](../battles/camp.md) broke the invasion and made Eärnil a leading candidate for the vacant throne.

---

## Outcome and Significance

Eärnil's victory ended the immediate invasion, but it did not restore Gondor's earlier influence in Rhovanion or repair all the losses suffered there. The death of Ondoher and his heirs created a succession crisis; Eärnil was chosen as king, while the claim of Arvedui of Arthedain was set aside.

The wars show how Gondor could win a decisive battle yet remain strategically weakened by repeated pressure on distant borders. The [Battle of the Plains](../battles/plains.md) and [Battle of the Camp](../battles/camp.md) are separate turning points within the longer conflict.
