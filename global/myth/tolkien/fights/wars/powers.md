# War of the Powers

*The Valar's campaign against Melkor · Years of the Trees*

---

## Overview

The War of the Powers was the Valar's campaign against Melkor after the awakening of the Elves at Cuiviénen. Melkor's servants had begun to capture and terrify the Firstborn, so the Valar went to Middle-earth to confront him. Their forces broke into Utumno, captured Melkor, and took him to Aman as a prisoner.

The victory removed Melkor from Middle-earth for a long captivity, but did not erase the danger he had created. Sauron escaped, and the division between Elves who journeyed west and those who remained shaped the history of the First Age.

## Causes and Context

The Elves awoke beside Cuiviénen while the Valar were still recovering from Melkor's earlier wars and the destruction of the Lamps. Melkor learned of their awakening before the Valar reached them. His servants spread fear and some of the Firstborn were taken away; the Valar later understood that Melkor had corrupted them.

The Valar debated how to protect the Elves. Rather than leave them under Melkor's influence, they resolved to challenge his power in Middle-earth. The war was therefore an intervention by the Valar on behalf of the Firstborn, not a conflict between the Elven kingdoms that came later.

---

## Course of the War

1. **The Valar march against Melkor.** The Valar came to Middle-earth with their host and advanced on the north, where Melkor had established Utumno as his chief stronghold.

2. **Utumno is assaulted.** The war lasted a long time and devastated the lands around Melkor's fortress. The Valar broke through Utumno's defenses and captured Melkor.

3. **Melkor is taken to Aman.** Melkor was bound and removed from Middle-earth. His lieutenant Sauron escaped, and Angband was not wholly destroyed, leaving a remnant of danger behind.

4. **The Elves are summoned west.** With Melkor in captivity, the Valar invited the Elves to journey to Aman. Some accepted; others remained in Middle-earth, beginning the division between the Eldar who went west and the Sindar who stayed.

The event is also catalogued as the [Battle of the Powers](../battles/powers.md), which focuses on the assault on Utumno rather than the wider campaign.

## Outcome and Significance

Melkor's capture began a long period in which he was held in Aman. The Elves' journey west followed, but the invitation did not bring all the Firstborn together: some refused or turned back, and many remained in Middle-earth. That division shaped the later political and cultural landscape of Beleriand.

The Valar's victory was not complete destruction of Melkor's power. Sauron escaped, and the surviving fortress of Angband later became the base from which Morgoth returned to war. The War of the Powers should not be confused with the [War of Wrath](./wrath.md), the final campaign against Morgoth at the end of the First Age.
