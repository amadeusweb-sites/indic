# War of the Ring

*The final war against Sauron · 3018–3019 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The War of the Ring was Sauron's attempt to recover the One Ring and bring the free peoples of Middle-earth under his rule. The conflict opened in Gondor, spread across Rohan and the northern realms, and culminated in the assault on Minas Tirith and the march to the Black Gate. While armies fought on several fronts, Frodo and Sam carried the Ring into Mordor to destroy it.

The Ring's destruction ended Sauron's power and caused his armies to collapse. The war was won not by a conquest of Mordor but by the success of the secret mission that Sauron failed to perceive.

## Causes and Context

Sauron had rebuilt his strength in Mordor and sought the Ring, which had been lost in the Anduin after Isildur's death. His servants searched for it while he prepared for war. The Ring came to Bilbo Baggins and then to Frodo, whose journey to destroy it became the hidden center of the conflict.

Sauron attacked several peoples at once to prevent them from uniting. Saruman, once an ally of the White Council, also sought the Ring and waged war against Rohan. The West had to defend its lands while keeping Sauron from learning the Ring-bearer's true purpose.

---

## Course of the War

1. **The war begins in Gondor.** Sauron's forces attacked Osgiliath in 3018, testing Gondor's defenses and crossing the Anduin. The bridge was destroyed, but the eastern side of the city was lost.

2. **Rohan is attacked from Isengard.** Saruman's forces struck at the Fords of Isen and then besieged the Rohirrim at the Hornburg. Théoden's people survived the [Battle of the Hornburg](../battles/hornburg.md), while the Ents attacked Isengard and destroyed its outer defenses.

3. **Minas Tirith is besieged.** Sauron's main army crossed the Anduin and surrounded Gondor's capital. Rohan answered the call for aid, and the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../battles/pelennor-fields.md) broke the siege.

4. **The northern realms fight their own battles.** Easterlings attacked Dale and Erebor, while Sauron's forces assaulted Lórien and Mirkwood. These fronts held until the fall of Sauron changed the course of the war.

5. **The West marches to the Black Gate.** Aragorn led a small army to the Morannon to draw Sauron's attention away from Frodo and Sam. The [Battle of the Morannon](../battles/morannon.md) began as a diversion against overwhelming odds.

6. **The Ring is destroyed.** Frodo and Sam reached Mount Doom. When the Ring was destroyed, Sauron's power collapsed, his armies lost their purpose, and the battles across Middle-earth ended.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The Ring's destruction ended Sauron's power and broke the armies that depended on him. Aragorn was crowned King Elessar, the kingdoms of Gondor and Arnor were reunited, and Rohan's alliance with Gondor endured. The northern defenders also survived the attacks on Dale, Erebor, Lórien, and Mirkwood.

The military victories were essential, but none could have ended the war while the Ring remained. The defense of Minas Tirith and the march to the Morannon bought time for Frodo and Sam; the decisive act took place at Mount Doom. The Battle of Bywater and the Scouring of the Shire followed the main war as Saruman's remaining influence was removed.
