# Kin-strife

*Gondor's civil war over the succession · 1432–1447 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Kin-strife was Gondor's civil war after the death of King Valacar. Castamir and his supporters rejected Eldacar as king because his mother, Vidumavi, was from the Northmen of Rhovanion. Eldacar was driven into exile, but later returned with northern allies, defeated Castamir at the Crossings of Erui, and restored his rule. The surviving rebels escaped from Pelargir to Umbar, where they became a continuing enemy of Gondor.

## Causes and Context

Valacar's marriage to Vidumavi had brought Gondor into closer contact with the Northmen, but some of the Dúnedain feared that Eldacar's mixed ancestry would weaken the royal line. Those opponents used his descent as a reason to challenge his claim after Valacar died in 1432. Castamir, a powerful captain of the fleet, led the rebellion.

The war was not only a dynastic dispute. Castamir's faction also drew support from those who favored the fleet and the southern ports over Osgiliath, the old capital. The fighting devastated Osgiliath and divided the kingdom; Eldacar's exile did not end his claim or the loyalty of all Gondorians.

---

## Course of the War

1. **Castamir challenges Eldacar.** After Valacar's death, Castamir's supporters rebelled. The civil war began in Gondor, and Osgiliath suffered severe destruction during the fighting.

2. **Eldacar is driven into exile.** Castamir seized the throne and Eldacar fled to Rhovanion, his mother's homeland. Castamir's rule did not reconcile the kingdom; his violence and disregard for the old capital increased opposition.

3. **Eldacar returns.** In 1447 Eldacar came back with an army from Rhovanion and met Castamir's forces at the Crossings of Erui. Eldacar defeated the rebels and killed Castamir. This decisive engagement is covered in the [Battle of the Crossings of Erui](../battles/erui.md).

4. **The rebels hold Pelargir.** Castamir's sons and surviving followers retreated to Pelargir and continued their resistance. Eldacar's forces pursued them; after the port fell, the rebels escaped by sea to Umbar. The final phase is described in the [Battle of Pelargir](../battles/pelargir.md).

## Outcome and Significance

Eldacar regained the throne, but the Kin-strife weakened Gondor and left deep divisions among its people. Castamir's sons and many followers escaped to Umbar, where they formed a hostile power that later threatened Gondor by sea. The civil war therefore ended within the kingdom but continued to shape its external conflicts.

The chronology matters: Castamir was killed at Erui, not at Pelargir. Pelargir was the rebels' last stronghold in Gondor before their flight to Umbar.
