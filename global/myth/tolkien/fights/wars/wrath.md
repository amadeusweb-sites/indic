# War of Wrath

*The final war against Morgoth · 545–587 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The War of Wrath was the final campaign against Morgoth. Eärendil's voyage to Aman brought the appeal of Elves and Men to the Valar, who sent the Host of the West to overthrow the Dark Enemy. Led by Eönwë, the host fought Morgoth's armies for decades, broke into Angband, and defeated him.

Morgoth was taken captive and removed from the world, but the victory devastated Beleriand. The land was broken and much of it sank beneath the sea; the Silmarils were not recovered as a set.

## Causes and Context

After the defeat of the Noldor and their allies at the Nirnaeth Arnoediad, Morgoth dominated much of Beleriand. Nargothrond and Gondolin fell, and Doriath was ruined. The surviving peoples could no longer defeat Angband through their own strength.

Eärendil reached Aman bearing a Silmaril and pleaded for aid on behalf of both Elves and Men. The Valar answered by sending a host west to Middle-earth. The war was not simply a continuation of the earlier Noldorin campaigns: it brought the power of Aman directly against Morgoth's rule.

---

## Course of the War

1. **Eärendil reaches Aman.** His voyage carried the appeal for help to the Valar. The Silmaril he bore became a sign of the struggle and was set in the heavens after the war.

2. **The Host of the West arrives.** Eönwë led the host into Beleriand. The Vanyar and many Noldor who had remained in Aman joined the campaign. The armies of Morgoth met them in a conflict that lasted for years.

3. **Angband is overthrown.** The Host of the West defeated Morgoth's forces and broke into Angband. Ancalagon the Black was slain by Eärendil in the air; his fall shattered the towers of Thangorodrim. Morgoth was captured and his remaining power destroyed.

4. **The Silmarils are taken and lost.** The jewels were recovered from Morgoth's crown, but the surviving sons of Fëanor stole two of them from the host. Maedhros and Maglor could not keep the jewels: one was lost in the earth and one in the sea. Eärendil retained the third in the heavens.

5. **Beleriand is broken.** The war changed the shape of the western lands. Survivors fled east or sailed away, and much of Beleriand was lost beneath the sea.

## Outcome and Significance

Morgoth was defeated, bound, and cast out of the world, ending his rule in Middle-earth. The victory did not restore the realms destroyed during the First Age. Beleriand's ruin forced the surviving Elves and Men into a new geography and a new political order.

The fate of the Silmarils completed the Oath of Fëanor's tragedy: the jewels were not returned to their makers or gathered again. The Edain who had aided the Elves were rewarded with Númenor, while many Elves later sailed west. The war closed the First Age and set the stage for the Second.
