# Wulf's Invasion of Rohan

*The Dunlending conquest and liberation of Rohan · 2758–2759 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

Wulf's invasion was an attack on Rohan by Dunlendings led by Wulf, son of Freca. After Helm Hammerhand killed Freca, Wulf gathered support among the Dunlendings and invaded Rohan during the Long Winter. The Rohirrim were defeated, Edoras was captured, and Helm's people took refuge in the Hornburg. Helm and his sons died, but his nephew Fréaláf retook Edoras and killed Wulf, restoring the kingdom.

## Causes and Context

Freca, a powerful lord of mixed Dunlending and Rohirric descent, sought a marriage between his son Wulf and Helm's daughter. Helm rejected the demand, and when Freca threatened him, Helm struck him a fatal blow. Wulf fled west and later used his father's death to unite the Dunlendings against Rohan.

The invasion coincided with the Long Winter of 2758–2759 and attacks by the Corsairs on Gondor. Rohan was isolated from the aid it might otherwise have received, while snow, hunger, and exposure made defense more difficult.

---

## Course of the War

1. **Wulf invades.** The Dunlendings crossed into Rohan in 2758 and defeated the Rohirrim in battle. Wulf captured Edoras and proclaimed himself king.

2. **Helm withdraws to the Hornburg.** Helm Hammerhand and his people took refuge in the fortress of the Hornburg. The Dunlendings occupied the Westfold, while the defenders endured a winter of hunger and repeated attacks.

3. **Helm and his sons die.** Helm's sons Haleth and Háma were killed during the war. Helm himself continued to fight through the winter, but died before the recovery of Edoras. His end gave rise to the later name Helm's Deep for the refuge where his people had survived.

4. **Fréaláf leads the counterattack.** Helm's nephew Fréaláf gathered support and retook Edoras. He killed Wulf in the fighting, ending the invasion and restoring the Rohirric kingship.

---

## Outcome and Significance

Fréaláf's victory ended Wulf's rule, but Rohan had suffered extensive loss of life and property. The Long Winter compounded the damage, and the rebuilding of the kingdom continued after the fighting stopped. Fréaláf became king and began a new line of rulers.

The war explains the name of Helm's Deep and the enduring hostility between the Rohirrim and the Dunlendings. It also shows that Rohan faced a major invasion generations before the [Battle of the Hornburg](../battles/hornburg.md) in the War of the Ring.
