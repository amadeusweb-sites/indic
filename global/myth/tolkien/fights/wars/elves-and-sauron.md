# War of the Elves and Sauron

*Sauron's invasion of Eregion and Eriador · 1693–1701 of the Second Age*

---

## Overview

The War of the Elves and Sauron began when Sauron, seeking control of the Rings of Power, turned against the Elves of Eregion. He invaded the realm, captured Ost-in-Edhil, and killed Celebrimbor, but did not recover the Three Rings. Elrond escaped with survivors and established Imladris. A Númenórean fleet sent by Tar-Minastir later joined Gil-galad's forces and drove Sauron from Eriador.

## Causes and Context

Sauron had helped the smiths of Eregion under the name Annatar, concealing his identity while encouraging the making of the Rings. The Elves made the Three without his direct knowledge, but Sauron forged the One Ring to dominate the others. When the Elves perceived his purpose and removed their Rings, he demanded that they surrender them. Celebrimbor refused, and Sauron turned to war.

Eregion lay between Sauron's growing power in Mordor and the Elven realms of Lindon. Its fall threatened the whole of Eriador, including the route to the Grey Havens. The conflict drew Númenor into Middle-earth's wars for the first time on a large scale.

---

## Course of the War

1. **Sauron attacks Eregion.** In 1693 the war began. Sauron's forces advanced into Eriador, and the Elves struggled to hold a front while seeking aid from Gil-galad.

2. **Eregion is overrun.** Sauron's army reached Ost-in-Edhil in 1697. Celebrimbor was captured and killed; Eregion ceased to exist as an independent realm. Elrond led survivors north and founded Imladris as a refuge.

3. **Sauron presses west.** Sauron continued his advance through Eriador, threatening the remaining Elven lands and the Grey Havens. The Three Rings had been sent away, so he did not gain possession of them, but he recovered other Rings from Eregion.

4. **Númenor sends aid.** Tar-Minastir dispatched a fleet and army under Ciryatur. The Númenórean forces landed in the south and moved up the Gwathló, while Gil-galad's Elves pressed from the west.

5. **Sauron is driven back.** The Elves and Númenóreans defeated Sauron's army near the Gwathló. Sauron escaped with a small following and returned to Mordor. The war ended in 1701, with Eriador relieved but Eregion permanently lost.

The fall of the city is described separately in the [Sack of Ost-in-Edhil](../sacks/ost-in-edhil.md), and the later relief is covered in the [Battle of the Gwathló](../battles/gwathlo.md).

---

## Outcome and Significance

Sauron's defeat near the Gwathló ended his immediate control of Eriador, but it did not restore Eregion or its people. Elrond's refuge at Imladris preserved an Elven presence in the region, while Gil-galad remained in Lindon. The Three Rings stayed beyond Sauron's reach; the surviving Elves continued to use them in secret.

The war marked the beginning of Númenor's direct military intervention in Middle-earth. Its success established Númenor as a major power against Sauron, beginning a rivalry that shaped the later Second Age and ended only with Númenor's downfall.
