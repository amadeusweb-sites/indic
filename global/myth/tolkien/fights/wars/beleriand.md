# Wars of Beleriand

*The wars against Morgoth in the west · c. 1497 of the Years of the Trees–587 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Wars of Beleriand were the successive conflicts between Morgoth and the peoples resisting his rule in the west of Middle-earth. They began with Morgoth's attacks on the Sindar before the Noldor returned from Aman, and continued through the Noldorin kingdoms, the alliance with the Edain, and the final intervention of the Valar. The wars were not one continuous front: they included long periods of siege, sudden defeats, the fall of hidden realms, and several distinct assaults on Doriath.

The conflict ended with Morgoth's defeat in the War of Wrath, but Beleriand was largely broken and lost beneath the sea. Individual battles, sieges, and sacks have separate entries; this page follows the wider sequence.

## Causes and Context

Morgoth returned to Middle-earth after stealing the Silmarils and killing Finwë. Fëanor and his sons pursued him under the Oath of Fëanor, while Fingolfin and other Noldor sought to establish realms and resist Morgoth's power. The Sindar, led by Thingol and Círdan, had already faced the first Orc assaults before the Noldor arrived.

The resistance never formed a stable united front. The Noldorin houses disagreed over leadership and the Silmarils; the Sindar guarded their own realms; and the Edain entered the conflict as they migrated into Beleriand. Morgoth exploited these divisions, sometimes attacking openly and at other times waiting behind Angband's defenses.

---

## Course of the Wars

1. **The Sindar face the first assaults.** Morgoth's Orcs attacked Beleriand before the Noldor returned. Doriath endured under Melian's protection, while Círdan's havens in the Falas were besieged. This opening phase is covered in the [First Battle of Beleriand](../battles/beleriand.md).

2. **The Noldor arrive and take the field.** Fëanor's host defeated the Orcs at Dagor-nuin-Giliath, but Fëanor was mortally wounded in his pursuit of them. Later, the Noldor's victory at Dagor Aglareb began the [Siege of Angband](../sieges/angband.md).

3. **Morgoth breaks the siege.** In 455, the Dagor Bragollach devastated the northern lands and ended the containment of Angband. The surviving Noldor and their allies lost much of their territory, but resistance continued in Hithlum, Himring, Doriath, and Nargothrond.

4. **The Union of Maedhros is defeated.** The Nirnaeth Arnoediad in 472 was a final attempt to unite the peoples of Beleriand against Morgoth. Betrayal, delay, and Morgoth's reserves broke the allied host and left the enemy dominant across the North.

5. **The hidden realms fall.** Nargothrond was destroyed after the Battle of Tumhalad; Doriath was devastated in successive conflicts over the Silmaril; and Gondolin fell after its location was betrayed. The survivors gathered at the Havens of Sirion, which were attacked in the Third Kinslaying.

6. **The Host of the West intervenes.** Eärendil's voyage brought the appeal for aid to Aman. The War of Wrath defeated Morgoth and ended his rule, but the conflict broke the lands of Beleriand and changed the western shores of Middle-earth.

## Outcome and Significance

Morgoth's defeat ended the First Age's central war, but it did not restore the realms that had fallen. The Noldorin kingdoms were broken, Doriath and Gondolin were gone, and the survivors of Beleriand had to leave a land being destroyed. The Silmarils remained beyond recovery as a united set: one was carried by Eärendil, while the other two were seized by the surviving sons of Fëanor and lost with them.

The wars also shaped the histories of Men and Elves beyond Beleriand. The Edain who survived were rewarded with Númenor, while many Elves later sailed west. The great battles—especially [Dagor Bragollach](../battles/dagor-bragollach.md) and [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../battles/nirnaeth-arnoediad.md)—are turning points within this larger conflict, not interchangeable names for the whole war.
