# Battle of Sarn Athrad

*Beren's ambush of the Dwarves of Nogrod · 503 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of Sarn Athrad followed the Dwarves of Nogrod's sack of Menegroth. The Dwarves were carrying away the Nauglamír and the Silmaril after killing Thingol. Beren gathered Elves of Ossiriand and intercepted them at Sarn Athrad, the ford of the Gelion. The Dwarves were defeated and the treasure recovered.

## Causes and Context

The Dwarves of Nogrod had made the Nauglamír for Thingol, who set a Silmaril into the necklace. When Thingol refused to surrender the jewel, the Dwarves killed him and sacked Menegroth. They then carried the treasure east toward the Blue Mountains.

Beren gathered the Green-elves of Ossiriand and pursued the returning host. The ford at Sarn Athrad gave him an opportunity to intercept the Dwarves before they reached their homeland. The battle was retaliation for the sack and an attempt to recover the stolen treasure; it did not restore Doriath or bring Thingol back.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Dwarves leave Menegroth.** After killing Thingol and sacking the city, they carried the Nauglamír and the Silmaril east.
2. **Beren gathers the Green-elves.** He rallied the people of Ossiriand and followed the Dwarves toward the Gelion.
3. **The Dwarves are intercepted at the ford.** Beren's force attacked at Sarn Athrad as the host crossed the river.
4. **The Dwarves are defeated.** Their lord was killed, and only a small number escaped toward the Blue Mountains.
5. **The treasure is recovered.** Beren took the Nauglamír and Silmaril and returned the jewel to Lúthien.

## Outcome and Significance

Beren recovered the Silmaril, but the battle did not repair the ruin of Doriath. The recovery instead carried the jewel into the next stage of its history: the sons of Fëanor later demanded it from Dior, Thingol's heir, and the demand led to another assault on Doriath.

Sarn Athrad is one stage in the [Sack of Menegroth](../sacks/menegroth.md), not a restoration of Thingol's kingdom. Its consequences show how the Silmaril continued to provoke conflict even after Beren and Lúthien recovered it.
