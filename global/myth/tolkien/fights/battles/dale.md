# Battle of Dale

*The defense of Dale and Erebor · 17 March 3019*

---

## Overview

The Battle of Dale was fought when Easterling forces attacked the Kingdom of Dale during the War of the Ring. King Brand defended the city with help from King Dáin Ironfoot and the Dwarves of Erebor. Both kings were killed, and the survivors withdrew into the Lonely Mountain, where they endured a siege.

## Causes and Context

Sauron's northern campaign was coordinated with the assault on Gondor. The Easterlings attacked Dale and Erebor while Sauron's main force moved against Minas Tirith, keeping the northern kingdoms from sending aid west. Dale and Erebor were allies, and Brand and Dáin fought together against the invasion.

The Easterlings drove the defenders from Dale after several days of fighting. The city was lost, but its fall did not end the resistance: the Men of Dale and the Dwarves withdrew into Erebor and continued to defend themselves.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Easterlings attack Dale.** Sauron's forces advanced against the city and the surrounding northern lands.
2. **Brand and Dáin defend the city.** The king of Dale and the Dwarf-king fought together against the invading host.
3. **The defenders are driven back.** After days of fighting, Brand and Dáin fell outside the gates of Erebor. Their people withdrew into the Mountain.
4. **The siege begins.** The Easterlings surrounded Erebor, where the surviving Men and Dwarves held out.
5. **The northern allies counterattack.** After Sauron's defeat, the besiegers lost their purpose and were defeated. Bard II succeeded Brand, and Thorin Stonehelm succeeded Dáin.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The battle cost Dale and Erebor their kings, but the survivors preserved both peoples. The retreat into the Mountain turned the battle into a siege, and the northern defenders endured until news of Sauron's fall reached them. The Easterlings were then defeated and the siege was lifted.

The attack shows the geographical reach of the War of the Ring. Even while the central struggle took place around Gondor and Mordor, Sauron's allies attacked the northern kingdoms to keep them occupied. The related [Battle of Erebor](./erebor.md) covers the Dwarves' defense and the siege from the Mountain's perspective.

## Editorial Note

The battle in Dale and the subsequent siege of Erebor are connected but distinct phases. Brand and Dáin were killed before the defenders withdrew into the Mountain.
