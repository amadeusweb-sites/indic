# Battle of the Five Armies

*The battle before the Lonely Mountain · 23 November 2941 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of the Five Armies was fought near the Lonely Mountain after the return of Thorin Oakenshield and his company to Erebor. A dispute over the recovered treasure brought the Dwarves into conflict with Bard and the Men of Lake-town and the Elvenking's host. Before the dispute was settled, a Goblin and Warg army descended from the north, forcing the former rivals to fight together.

The five armies named in the battle were the Goblins, Wargs, Elves, Men, and Dwarves. The Eagles and Beorn arrived as allies during the fighting. The victory cost Thorin, Fíli, and Kíli their lives, but it restored the Dwarven kingdom under the Mountain and led to Bard's restoration of Dale.

## Causes and Context

Thorin's company had reclaimed Erebor and its treasure from Smaug, but the dragon's attack on Lake-town had left many people homeless. Bard, who had killed Smaug, sought a share of the treasure to help the survivors and to restore Dale. The Elvenking came with him, supporting Bard's claim and seeking a share for his people.

Thorin refused their demands and fortified the Mountain. Bilbo, hoping to prevent a battle between the Dwarves and their neighbors, secretly gave Bard the Arkenstone as a bargaining token. Thorin regarded this as betrayal and still would not yield. Dáin Ironfoot then arrived from the Iron Hills with a Dwarven army.

Before the dispute could turn into open war, scouts reported that a large Goblin and Warg host was approaching from the Misty Mountains. The old enmity between the Dwarves and Goblins, and the losses inflicted on the Goblins during the journey to Erebor, made the Mountain a target. The Elves, Men, and Dwarves set aside their conflict to meet the common threat.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The rivals prepare to fight.** Bard and the Elvenking faced Thorin's company outside the Mountain, while Dáin and his Dwarves approached from the east. The Arkenstone remained with Bard, and Thorin rejected the settlement Bilbo had hoped to secure.

2. **The Goblin host appears.** The approaching army, led by Bolg, came down from the north with Wargs. The Elves, Men, and Dwarves recognized that none of them could withstand the attack alone and formed a common defense.

3. **The allied forces take the field.** The Elves and Men held a position on the slopes, while the Dwarves fought from the Mountain and Dale. The narrow ground shaped the battle: the allies tried to contain the Goblins, but the enemy's numbers pressed them back.

4. **Thorin leads a sortie.** Thorin emerged from the Mountain with his company and charged the Goblin ranks. Fíli and Kíli fought beside him and were killed. Thorin was mortally wounded; his attack helped break the enemy's advance but could not save him.

5. **The Eagles and Beorn arrive.** The Eagles descended upon the battlefield, attacking the Goblins from above. Beorn entered the fight in bear-shape, rescued the wounded Thorin, and killed Bolg. With their leader dead and the allied forces reinforced, the Goblins were routed.

6. **The survivors settle the dispute.** Thorin made peace with Bilbo before he died. Dáin became King under the Mountain, Bard received a share of the treasure for the people of Lake-town, and the Elvenking and the Men of Lake-town were also given a portion. Bilbo accepted a modest share and began his journey home.

## Outcome and Significance

The allied forces defeated the Goblin and Warg host. Thorin, Fíli, and Kíli died, while Dáin succeeded Thorin as King under the Mountain. Bard used his share of the treasure to rebuild Dale, and the people of Lake-town received aid. The Elvenking's share helped settle the immediate dispute.

The battle also marks the point at which Bilbo's role changes from burglar to peacemaker: giving up the Arkenstone put him at odds with Thorin, but Thorin reconciled with him before dying. Bilbo returned to the Shire with a small portion of the treasure and a lasting friendship with the Dwarves.

This 2941 battle is distinct from the later [Battle of Dale](./dale.md) and [Battle of Erebor](./erebor.md), fought during the War of the Ring in 3019.

## Editorial Note

The five armies are the Goblins, Wargs, Elves, Men, and Dwarves; the Eagles and Beorn are reinforcements rather than additional armies in the title. The account follows Bilbo's limited point of view in *The Hobbit* and does not treat the later battles at Dale and Erebor as part of this event.
