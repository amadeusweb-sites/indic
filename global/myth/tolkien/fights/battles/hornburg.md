# Battle of the Hornburg

*The defense of Helm's Deep · 3–4 March 3019*

---

## Overview

The Battle of the Hornburg was Rohan's defense of the fortress at Helm's Deep against Saruman's army. Théoden brought his force there after the West-mark had been defeated at the Fords of Isen. The defenders held through the night; at dawn Théoden and Aragorn rode out as Gandalf arrived with Erkenbrand and the gathered men of the Westfold.

## Causes and Context

Saruman's forces had broken Rohan's western defense at the [Fords of Isen](./fords-of-isen--second.md). Théoden then rode west to confront the invasion, but his force was too small to meet Saruman's army in open country. The Hornburg, part of the fortress complex at Helm's Deep, offered a defensible position where the king could shelter the people of the Westfold.

The defense was not an isolated battle. The Rohirrim at the fortress had been weakened by the losses at the fords, while Erkenbrand was gathering scattered survivors. At the same time, the Ents were marching on Isengard, Saruman's stronghold.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Rohirrim take shelter.** Théoden's force and the people of the Westfold gathered at Helm's Deep and the Hornburg as Saruman's army approached.
2. **Saruman's army assaults the defenses.** Orcs and other troops attacked the Deeping Wall and the Hornburg through the night. Aragorn and Éomer fought alongside the defenders.
3. **The Deeping Wall is breached.** An explosive device broke the wall, allowing the attackers into the Deeping-coomb. The defenders withdrew toward the Hornburg and continued to resist.
4. **The defenders make a sortie.** At dawn, Théoden and Aragorn rode out from the fortress. Their attack met the arrival of Gandalf and Erkenbrand with the men gathered from the Westfold.
5. **The enemy is trapped.** The Huorns cut off the retreating army. Saruman's force was destroyed or scattered, and the survivors could no longer threaten Rohan as an organized host.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The defense saved Théoden's force and halted Saruman's invasion of Rohan. The victory relied on the defenders holding the fortress long enough for the counterattack and reinforcements to arrive; the Huorns then prevented the enemy from escaping as an army.

The Ents' assault on Isengard was a parallel action, not part of the fighting at the Hornburg. Together, the two events broke Saruman's military power: his field army was defeated at Helm's Deep, and his stronghold was flooded by the Ents. The surviving Rohirrim later rode to Gondor's aid at the [Pelennor Fields](./pelennor-fields.md).

## Editorial Note
