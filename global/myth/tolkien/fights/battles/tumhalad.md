# Battle of Tumhalad

*The destruction of Nargothrond's field army · 495 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of Tumhalad was the decisive defeat of Nargothrond's army by Morgoth's forces. Orodreth led the host into open battle against Glaurung and the Orcs. The narrow valley restricted the Elves' movement, and the army was overwhelmed. Orodreth and Gwindor fell; Túrin survived and returned to Nargothrond.

## Causes and Context

Nargothrond had long relied on secrecy and the defenses of the Narog. Túrin urged Orodreth to build a bridge over the river and fight Morgoth openly. This policy made the kingdom's strength and location easier for the enemy to discover. When Glaurung advanced, Orodreth chose to meet him in the field rather than remain behind the river and the city's defenses.

The battle's defeat led directly to the fall of Nargothrond, but the fighting and the sack are separate events. The [Sack of Nargothrond](../sacks/nargothrond.md) followed after the army had been broken.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Nargothrond marches out.** Orodreth led the city's army, with Túrin and Gwindor among its warriors, to confront Glaurung.
2. **The armies meet at Tumhalad.** The Elves fought in a confined valley where their formation and ability to maneuver were limited.
3. **Glaurung breaks the host.** The dragon and the Orcs overwhelmed the Nargothrondrim. Orodreth was killed, and Gwindor was mortally wounded.
4. **Túrin survives the rout.** He escaped with the few who remained and returned toward the city.
5. **Nargothrond is left undefended.** Glaurung advanced into the city, beginning its sack and the destruction of the kingdom.

## Outcome and Significance

The defeat destroyed Nargothrond's ability to defend itself in the field. Orodreth's death ended his rule, and the loss of the army left the city open to Glaurung. The battle was the immediate military cause of the sack, although the vulnerability of the kingdom had grown over time as its defenses were made more visible.

Túrin's survival is essential to the next part of the story. He returned to Nargothrond, where Glaurung's influence and deception diverted him from rescuing Finduilas. The battle therefore marks both the end of Nargothrond's organized resistance and a turning point in the history of Túrin.

## Editorial Note

Túrin fought at Tumhalad and survived; he was not absent from the battle. The destruction of the army and the subsequent sack of Nargothrond are treated as separate events.
