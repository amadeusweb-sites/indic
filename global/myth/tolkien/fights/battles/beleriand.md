# First Battle of Beleriand

*The first great assault on the Sindar · 1497 of the Years of the Trees*

---

## Overview

The First Battle of Beleriand was Morgoth's first major assault on the Sindar after his return to Middle-earth. Orcs attacked the Green-elves of Ossiriand, Thingol's people in the interior, and Círdan's coastal settlements in the Falas. The Sindar survived, but the battle scattered communities, killed Denethor, and left the havens under siege.

## Causes and Context

Morgoth returned to Middle-earth and rebuilt his stronghold in Angband. Before the Noldor returned from Aman, the Sindar and other Elves of Beleriand faced his growing power without the large Noldorin kingdoms that later defended the region. Morgoth's Orcs moved against the Elvish communities from the north and east.

This “First Battle” was not one clash on a single field. The attacks spread across a wide region and affected distinct peoples: the Green-elves of Ossiriand, Thingol's Sindar, and Círdan's coastal folk. The name is a later chronological distinction used to separate these attacks from the battles fought after the Noldor's return.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Orcs enter Ossiriand.** Morgoth's forces attacked the Green-elves under Denethor. The Green-elves were less equipped for open war and sought help from Thingol's people.
2. **Denethor is killed.** The Green-elves were besieged on Amon Ereb. Denethor fell, and his people withdrew into the woods rather than continue the exposed fight.
3. **Thingol's people are attacked.** Orcs pressed into Beleriand and scattered Sindarin communities. Thingol's forces resisted, but could not prevent losses across the open lands.
4. **The Falas is besieged.** Círdan's havens at Brithombar and Eglarest were attacked. The coastal Elves held their settlements but were isolated from the interior.
5. **The surviving realms endure.** Thingol withdrew to the protection of Doriath, while Círdan's people remained at the coast. The Noldor's later arrival changed the balance of power but did not restore those lost in the first assault.

## Outcome and Significance

The battle demonstrated that the Sindar could not defend every part of Beleriand against Morgoth's growing host. Denethor's death was a serious loss to the Green-elves, and Círdan's havens remained under siege. Yet the conflict did not destroy the Sindar: Doriath endured behind Melian's power, and the Falas continued to resist.

The later arrival of the Noldor brought a new military force and new political divisions. The [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](./dagor-nuin-giliath.md) belongs to this later phase and is not part of the First Battle. The First Battle is the opening conflict in the long sequence that would include the Siege of Angband and the later wars of Beleriand.

## Editorial Note

The First Battle is a series of attacks across Beleriand, not a single engagement with one front or one commander. The article separates the fate of Ossiriand, Thingol's lands, and the Falas rather than compressing them into one battlefield narrative.
