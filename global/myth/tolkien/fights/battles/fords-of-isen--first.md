# First Battle of the Fords of Isen

*The first defense of Rohan's western frontier · 25 February 3019*

---

## Overview

The First Battle of the Fords of Isen was Saruman's first major assault on Rohan. Théodred, son of Théoden and commander of the West-mark, tried to hold the crossings against an army advancing from Isengard. The Rohirrim were forced back, and Théodred died of his wounds on the island between the fords.

## Causes and Context

The Isen crossings guarded the western approach to Rohan. Saruman's forces gathered at Isengard while Théoden was weakened by Gríma's influence, leaving Théodred to command the West-mark and respond to the threat. The Rohirrim's defense at the river was intended to stop or delay an incursion before it reached the heart of the kingdom.

The battle began a pair of defeats that left Rohan's western defenses exposed. It is distinct from the Second Battle of the Fords of Isen, fought a week later after Théoden had ridden east with the main force.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Saruman's forces approach the crossings.** The Rohirrim learned that an army was advancing from Isengard and gathered to defend the river.
2. **Théodred leads the defense.** The West-mark forces under Théodred met the attackers at the fords. Fighting spread across the riverbanks and the island between the crossings.
3. **The Rohirrim are driven back.** Saruman's forces pressed across the Isen and forced the defenders from their positions.
4. **Théodred is mortally wounded.** He was struck down on the island. His men recovered him, but he died despite their efforts.
5. **The captains preserve the survivors.** Grimbold and Elfhelm continued the defense long enough for the remaining Rohirrim to withdraw. The crossings were not securely held, and Rohan's western army was left under severe pressure.

---

## Outcome and Significance

Théodred's death removed Théoden's only son and heir at a moment when Rohan faced invasion. The loss also weakened the command structure in the West-mark. Grimbold and Elfhelm survived to fight again, but the western defense was fragmented.

The defeat did not by itself decide the war. A second Isengard assault followed on 2 March, and the surviving Rohirrim then withdrew toward Helm's Deep. The events of that engagement are covered in the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](./fords-of-isen--second.md); its survivors helped explain why Théoden's force at the Hornburg was isolated.
