# Battle of the Gwathló

*The Númenórean relief of Eriador · 1700 of the Second Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of the Gwathló was the turning point in Sauron's War against the Elves of Eriador. After Sauron had overrun much of Eregion and pushed the Elves west, a Númenórean fleet sent by Tar-Minastir landed troops under Ciryatur. Gil-galad's forces and the Númenóreans converged on Sauron's army and defeated it near the Gwathló.

## Causes and Context

Sauron attacked Eregion to seize the Rings of Power and defeat the Elves of Eriador. The fall of Eregion left Lindon and the Grey Havens as the chief remaining Elvish strongholds in the west. Gil-galad asked Númenor for aid, and Tar-Minastir sent a fleet under Ciryatur.

The intervention was the first major military confrontation between Númenor and Sauron. Its success depended on coordinated pressure: Gil-galad's forces advanced from the north, while Ciryatur's Númenóreans arrived by sea and moved up the Gwathló.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Sauron advances west.** After the destruction of Eregion, his army moved toward the coast and the remaining Elvish havens.
2. **Númenor sends aid.** Ciryatur's fleet reached the coast with a large force sent by Tar-Minastir.
3. **The allies close on Sauron's host.** Gil-galad pressed from the north while Ciryatur's force advanced from the south and along the river.
4. **Sauron's army is trapped and defeated.** The Elves and Númenóreans drove the host toward the Gwathló and destroyed it.
5. **Sauron escapes.** He fled east with only a small following and returned to Mordor. His power in Eriador was broken, though he was not captured.

## Outcome and Significance

The defeat destroyed Sauron's army in Eriador and forced him back to Mordor. It relieved the Elves of the immediate threat and demonstrated Númenor's ability to project military power across the sea. Sauron escaped, however, and remained a threat in the Second Age.

The wider conflict is covered in the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../wars/elves-and-sauron.md). The Battle of the Gwathló was its turning point, not the end of Sauron's history or the final defeat of his power.
