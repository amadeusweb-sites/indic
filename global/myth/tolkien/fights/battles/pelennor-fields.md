# Battle of the Pelennor Fields

*The relief of Minas Tirith · 15 March 3019*

---

## Overview

The Battle of the Pelennor Fields was the decisive battle for Minas Tirith during the War of the Ring. Sauron's forces had crossed the Anduin, besieged the city, and breached its Great Gate. At dawn, Théoden's Rohirrim arrived from the north and broke the siege; later, Aragorn brought reinforcements from the south in the captured Corsair fleet.

## Causes and Context

The battle followed Sauron's assault on Gondor. His forces crossed the Anduin and laid siege to Minas Tirith, while the defenders were weakened by losses at Osgiliath and the Rammas Echor. Denethor commanded the defense until despair overcame him; Gandalf took charge as the city faced the final assault.

Rohan was bound to aid Gondor by the oath between Cirion and Eorl. Théoden gathered the Rohirrim and rode to Minas Tirith, taking a route that brought them to the Pelennor at dawn. At the same time, Aragorn was moving north from Pelargir with the captured Corsair ships and reinforcements from Gondor's southern fiefs.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Minas Tirith is besieged.** Sauron's army surrounded the city and used Grond to break the Great Gate. Gandalf and the defenders continued to resist inside the walls.
2. **The Rohirrim arrive.** Théoden's host reached the Pelennor at dawn and attacked the besieging army. Their charge broke the siege lines and drove the enemy across the fields.
3. **Théoden falls.** The Witch-king confronted the King of Rohan. Théoden was mortally wounded, and Éowyn—supported by Merry—fought the Witch-king and defeated him.
4. **The southern forces arrive.** Aragorn's ships came up the Anduin carrying the Grey Company and men from the southern fiefs. The arrival strengthened the defenders and changed the balance of the battle.
5. **The allied army defeats Sauron's host.** Éomer and Aragorn led the remaining forces against the enemy. Sauron's army was driven from the Pelennor, though the larger war was not yet over.
6. **The West prepares a diversion.** The surviving captains marched to the Black Gate to draw Sauron's attention away from Frodo and Sam. Their next engagement was the [Battle of the Morannon](./morannon.md).

## Outcome and Significance

The victory relieved Minas Tirith and destroyed the main force that had besieged it. It came at great cost: Théoden died, Denethor perished during the siege, and many defenders and allies were lost. Éowyn and Merry's defeat of the Witch-king fulfilled Glorfindel's ancient prophecy, while Aragorn's arrival with the southern forces made possible the final counterattack.

The battle did not end Sauron's power. The Ring remained in Mordor, and the West's captains chose to march to the Black Gate as a diversion for the Ring-bearer. The Pelennor was therefore the turning point in Gondor's defense, not the final battle of the War of the Ring.
