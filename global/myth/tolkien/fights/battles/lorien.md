# Battles of Lórien

*The assaults from Dol Guldur · March 3019*

---

## Overview

During the War of the Ring, Sauron's forces attacked Lórien from Dol Guldur three times. Galadriel and Celeborn's people defended the Golden Wood while the main war was fought in the west. The assaults failed to take Lórien, and after Sauron's fall the victors crossed the Anduin and entered Dol Guldur.

## Causes and Context

Dol Guldur stood on the eastern side of the Anduin and provided Sauron with a base close to Lórien. The attacks on the forest realm were part of the wider War of the Ring, timed while Sauron's main force besieged Minas Tirith and his allies fought in northern Mirkwood.

The published chronology records three assaults but does not provide a detailed account of their tactics, commanders, or losses. These battles should therefore be described as a sequence of attacks and defenses, not expanded into unsupported individual engagements.

---

## Course of the Battles

1. **The first assault begins.** Sauron's forces crossed the Anduin from Dol Guldur and attacked Lórien.
2. **The Galadhrim hold their defenses.** The attack failed to take the forest realm or Caras Galadhon.
3. **Further assaults follow.** Sauron's forces attacked twice more during March. The defenders resisted each assault.
4. **Sauron's power collapses.** The destruction of the One Ring ended his command over the war and removed the threat from Dol Guldur.
5. **The victors cross the river.** Celeborn led forces across the Anduin into Dol Guldur. Galadriel later destroyed its stronghold.

---

## Outcome and Significance

Lórien survived the assaults, but the forest's protection did not mean the war had passed it by. Its defenders were required to hold their own front while the Fellowship and the armies of the West dealt with the One Ring and Sauron's main forces.

After Sauron's fall, the Galadhrim crossed the Anduin and entered Dol Guldur. Galadriel cleansed the stronghold, ending the threat that had long stood opposite Lórien. The simultaneous attack on northern Mirkwood is described in the [Battle under the Trees](./mirkwood.md).
