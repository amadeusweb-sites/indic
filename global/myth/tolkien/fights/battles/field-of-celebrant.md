# Battle of the Field of Celebrant

*Eorl's aid to Gondor · 2510 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of the Field of Celebrant saved Gondor's northern army from the Balchoth. The Easterling invaders had crossed the Anduin and forced Gondor's forces back toward the Limlight. Cirion, Steward of Gondor, sent north for aid; Eorl the Young brought the Éothéod south and attacked the Balchoth at a critical moment.

## Causes and Context

The Balchoth crossed the Anduin in force and ravaged Calenardhon, a sparsely peopled province of Gondor. Gondor's northern army was driven back toward the Limlight and faced destruction. Cirion needed help from beyond the immediate frontier and sent a message north to the Éothéod.

Eorl and his people lived far to the north, so their response required a rapid ride across a great distance. The battle is the decisive encounter within the wider [War against the Balchoth](../wars/balchoth.md); that war page also covers the invasion and the political settlement that followed.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Balchoth advance.** Their army crossed the Anduin and pressed into Calenardhon, forcing Gondor's northern force toward the Limlight.
2. **Cirion sends for aid.** The Steward's appeal was carried north by Borondir, who reached Eorl and delivered the message.
3. **Eorl rides south.** Eorl led the Éothéod in a rapid mounted march to answer Gondor's call.
4. **The Éothéod arrive during the fighting.** Eorl's force attacked the Balchoth's flank while Gondor's army was still engaged.
5. **The invaders are routed.** The Balchoth were defeated and Gondor's northern army was saved.
6. **The alliance is formalized.** Cirion later granted Calenardhon to the Éothéod, and he and Eorl swore an oath of friendship and mutual aid at Amon Anwar.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The victory ended the immediate invasion and preserved Gondor's northern force. Cirion's grant of Calenardhon enabled the Éothéod to settle there as the Rohirrim. The oath at Amon Anwar established the alliance between Gondor and Rohan, which endured into the War of the Ring.

The battle and the war are related but not interchangeable: the Field of Celebrant was the decisive clash, while the wider invasion and settlement form the broader campaign. The later arrival of Rohan at the [Pelennor Fields](./pelennor-fields.md) reflects the obligations established by Cirion and Eorl.
