# Fall of Gondolin

*The destruction of the Hidden City · 510 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Fall of Gondolin was Morgoth's assault on the hidden city of Turgon. Gondolin had remained undiscovered for centuries, but Maeglin was captured beyond its borders and betrayed its location and defenses. Morgoth's host attacked by surprise, overwhelmed the city, and killed Turgon and many of its defenders. Tuor, Idril, and a company of survivors escaped.

## Causes and Context

Turgon had built Gondolin in a hidden valley and kept its location secret from Morgoth. The city survived the ruin of Beleriand for centuries because its enemies did not know where it lay. That protection failed after Maeglin was captured outside the city and, under threat and temptation, revealed its position and defenses.

The assault came in 510 of the First Age, during a festival when the inhabitants had little warning. The Fall of Gondolin was one of the last great losses of the Noldorin kingdoms before the [War of Wrath](../wars/wrath.md).

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Morgoth learns the city's location.** Maeglin's capture and betrayal gave Morgoth the information needed to prepare an attack.
2. **The host reaches Tumladen.** Morgoth's forces crossed the surrounding mountains and descended on the hidden city with Orcs, Balrogs, and dragons.
3. **Gondolin resists street by street.** The city's defenders fought fiercely, but the attack's surprise and scale overwhelmed the defenses.
4. **Turgon's champions fall.** Ecthelion slew Gothmog but died in the struggle. Glorfindel fought a Balrog during the escape and also perished.
5. **Turgon dies as the city falls.** Gondolin was destroyed, and the king did not survive the assault.
6. **Tuor and Idril lead survivors away.** A company escaped by a secret route to the south, carrying forward the line that would lead to Eärendil.

## Outcome and Significance

Gondolin's destruction eliminated one of the last major Noldorin realms in Beleriand. The death of Turgon and the loss of the city were catastrophic, but the escape of Tuor, Idril, and their company preserved survivors and the lineage that would produce Eärendil. His later voyage brought the intervention that led to Morgoth's defeat in the War of Wrath.

The battle also marks the cost of secrecy failing: Gondolin's defenses had protected it for centuries, but a single betrayal exposed the city. The event is not the final defeat of Morgoth; that came after the survivors reached the coast and Eärendil sought aid.
