# Battle of the Plains

*Gondor's defeat by the Wainriders · 1856 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of the Plains was a major defeat for Gondor in its first war with the Wainriders. King Narmacil II and Marhari, leader of the Northmen allied to Gondor, were killed. Gondor lost control of its eastern territories, and the Northmen of Rhovanion were left exposed to Wainrider rule.

## Causes and Context

The Wainriders were a powerful people from the East whose expansion threatened Gondor and its northern allies. Sauron encouraged the movement of eastern peoples against Gondor, though the surviving account does not describe the Wainriders' command structure in detail. Rhovanion, east of the Anduin, was difficult for Gondor to defend and depended on cooperation between the southern kingdom and the Northmen.

Narmacil II led Gondor's army into the conflict with the Northmen under Marhari fighting beside it. The battle ended in a decisive defeat: the king and Marhari died, and the Wainriders gained control over much of Rhovanion.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Gondor and the Northmen confront the Wainriders.** Narmacil II led the Gondorian force into Rhovanion, supported by Marhari and his people.
2. **The battle turns against the allies.** The Wainriders defeated the combined force. Tolkien's historical summary records the leaders' deaths and the territorial consequences rather than a detailed tactical narrative.
3. **Narmacil II and Marhari fall.** Their deaths weakened both Gondor's eastern defense and the Northmen's ability to resist.
4. **Gondor withdraws west of the Anduin.** The defeat ended Gondor's control of its territories east of the river and left Rhovanion under Wainrider pressure.
5. **The conflict continues.** Gondor later renewed its resistance under Calimehtar and, in a later generation, Eärnil. The Wainrider war did not end at the Plains; its final defeat came at the [Battle of the Camp](./camp.md) in 1944.

## Outcome and Significance

The defeat reduced Gondor's reach and ended its rule over lands east of the Anduin. The loss was not merely territorial: it broke the buffer between Gondor and the East and left the Northmen vulnerable. Marhari's son Marhwini survived and led the Northmen who escaped into the Vales of Anduin; their later history preserved a connection with Gondor.

Gondor eventually struck back. Calimehtar defeated the Wainriders at Dagorlad in 1899, but the wider threat persisted until Eärnil's victories in 1944. The Battle of the Plains is therefore the opening major defeat in a long conflict, not a standalone war's conclusion.
