# War of the Powers

*The Valar's campaign against Melkor · Years of the Trees*

---

## Overview

The War of the Powers was the Valar's campaign against Melkor after the awakening of the Elves. Seeking to protect the Firstborn from his corruption, the Valar crossed Middle-earth and attacked his stronghold in the north. The war broke Utumno, ended with Melkor captured and taken west as a prisoner, and reshaped the northern lands.

## Causes and Context

After the Elves awoke at Cuiviénen, Melkor sought to corrupt and capture some of them. The Valar learned of the danger and decided to remove Melkor's power from Middle-earth. This was an intervention by the Powers of Arda, not a conflict between mortal kingdoms.

The campaign's scale was immense. Tolkien describes the north being broken and changed by the struggle, but does not provide a conventional chronology or a list of battles. This page focuses on the assault on Utumno and Melkor's capture; the broader conflict and its consequences are also covered in the [War of the Powers](../wars/powers.md).

---

## Course of the War

1. **The Valar prepare for war.** They gathered their strength in Aman and came against Melkor's power in Middle-earth.
2. **The conflict reshapes the north.** The Valar's assault and Melkor's resistance broke and altered the lands around his strongholds.
3. **Utumno is attacked.** The Valar's host broke into Melkor's fortress after a prolonged campaign.
4. **Melkor is captured.** Tulkas overcame him, and the Valar bound him and took him west to face judgment.
5. **Melkor's servants remain a threat.** Sauron escaped the ruin of Utumno, and Angband was not wholly destroyed. The Valar's victory removed Melkor for an age but did not erase the forces that would later serve him.

## Outcome and Significance

The defeat and capture of Melkor changed the history of the Elves. With their enemy removed from Middle-earth, the Valar invited them to journey to Aman. Some accepted; others remained in Middle-earth, and their different choices shaped the later peoples and kingdoms of Beleriand.

The victory was not permanent peace. Sauron escaped, Angband remained, and Melkor returned to Middle-earth after his captivity. The War of the Powers therefore delayed the next phase of conflict rather than ending the struggle between the Valar and Melkor.
