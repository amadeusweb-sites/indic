# Battle of Greenfields

*The Shire's defense against Orcs · 2747 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of Greenfields was an Orc raid into the Shire, remembered as the most serious battle fought there before the War of the Ring. Bandobras Took, called the Bullroarer, rallied the Hobbit defenders and defeated the Orcs under Golfimbul.

## Causes and Context

The Orcs entered the Shire from the northern highlands, an unusual incursion into a land whose inhabitants were not known as a military people. The raid threatened the North Moors and the settlements beyond them. The surviving account does not describe a larger campaign or identify a political cause for the attack.

Bandobras Took was unusually tall and strong for a Hobbit. He became the central figure in the Shire's defense, turning a local response into a victory remembered in Hobbit history.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Orcs enter from the north.** Golfimbul led the invading force into the Shire.
2. **Bandobras rallies the defenders.** The Bullroarer gathered the Shire-folk and led them against the Orcs.
3. **The Orcs are defeated.** Bandobras killed Golfimbul and the invaders were driven out.
4. **A Hobbit tale grows from the victory.** Golfimbul's head was said to fly into a rabbit hole, inspiring the Shire's game of golf.

## Outcome and Significance

The victory ended the immediate Orc incursion and became a rare example of the Shire's people taking up arms before the War of the Ring. Its historical scale was small compared with the wars of the wider world, but its place in Hobbit memory was lasting.

The humorous golf story is part of the account's tone, not evidence that the battle was imaginary. It remains a recorded conflict in the Shire's chronology and a reminder that the North was not always free from danger.
