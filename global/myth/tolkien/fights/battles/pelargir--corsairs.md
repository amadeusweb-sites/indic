# Battle of Pelargir

*Aragorn's capture of the Corsair fleet · 13 March 3019*

---

## Overview

The Battle of Pelargir was fought when Aragorn's company reached the southern port through the Paths of the Dead. The Corsairs of Umbar were preparing to carry forces north for Sauron. The Dead Men of Dunharrow broke their resistance, and Aragorn captured the ships, which he then used to bring reinforcements to Minas Tirith.

## Causes and Context

Sauron's forces had besieged Minas Tirith, while the Corsairs of Umbar threatened to bring reinforcements up the Anduin. Aragorn chose the Paths of the Dead to reach southern Gondor in time to prevent the fleet from joining the siege. He was accompanied by the Grey Company and the Dead Men, whose oath to Isildur's heirs remained unfulfilled.

The battle's strategic importance lay in the ships as much as the port. The roads to Minas Tirith were watched, but the river offered Aragorn a way to bring a relief force to the city.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **Aragorn takes the Paths of the Dead.** He led the Grey Company through the mountain pass, calling on the Dead Men to fulfill their oath.
2. **The company reaches Pelargir.** The Corsairs were at the port with their fleet and forces gathered for the war.
3. **The Dead Men break the Corsairs' resistance.** The Corsairs fled in terror before the dead host. Aragorn did not need to destroy them in a conventional battle to seize the ships.
4. **Aragorn takes the fleet.** The captains and crews were compelled to serve; the men of the southern fiefs joined the living force.
5. **The ships sail north.** Aragorn brought the reinforcements up the Anduin, arriving at the Pelennor during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](./pelennor-fields.md).

## Outcome and Significance

Aragorn's seizure of the fleet reversed a threat into a means of relief. The Corsair ships that might have carried enemies to Minas Tirith instead brought Aragorn and reinforcements from the southern fiefs. Their arrival helped turn the tide on the Pelennor.

The episode also resolved the oath of the Dead Men of Dunharrow. After helping Aragorn at Pelargir, they were released from their service. This 3019 battle is separate from the [Battle of Pelargir during the Kin-strife](./pelargir.md), fought centuries earlier.
