# Ents' Assault on Isengard

*The destruction of Saruman's stronghold · 3019 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Ents' assault on Isengard followed Saruman's attack on Rohan. Treebeard and the Ents, roused by Merry and Pippin, marched against the fortress while Saruman's army was away at Helm's Deep. They broke the outer defenses and flooded Isengard, trapping Saruman in Orthanc.

## Causes and Context

Saruman had turned Isengard into an industrial fortress and used its surrounding lands for timber and war-making. The destruction of the trees and Saruman's attack on Rohan made the Ents' conflict with him immediate. Merry and Pippin brought the news of Saruman's plans to Treebeard, who called the Ents to an Entmoot.

The Ents chose to attack while Saruman's main army was away fighting at the [Hornburg](./hornburg.md). Isengard's walls and gates were formidable against ordinary attackers, but the Ents had the strength to break stone and alter the course of water.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The Ents decide to march.** After hearing Merry and Pippin's account and deliberating at Entmoot, Treebeard led the Ents against Isengard.
2. **The outer defenses are broken.** The Ents battered down the gates and tore apart sections of the ring-wall. Saruman's remaining defenders could not stop them.
3. **The Ents destroy the industrial works.** They dismantled the machinery and works Saruman had built, ending Isengard's role as a functioning war base.
4. **The waters are diverted.** The Ents broke the dams and redirected the Isen into the ring of Isengard. The flood covered the inner works and isolated Orthanc.
5. **Saruman is contained.** The Ents could not break the tower of Orthanc, but they surrounded it and kept Saruman from resuming the war.

## Outcome and Significance

The assault ended Saruman's ability to wage war from Isengard, but did not kill him or destroy Orthanc. The Ents kept him confined in the tower and later watched over him. The Ring of Isengard was ruined, and the industrial works that had damaged the surrounding land were destroyed.

The Ents' victory coincided with Saruman's defeat at the Hornburg. These two events broke his power by different means: Rohan defeated his field army, while the Ents eliminated his stronghold. The meeting of the victors at Isengard is described in the chapters following the battle.

## Editorial Note

The Ents flooded Isengard and trapped Saruman; they did not break Orthanc or kill him. The battle is separate from the Rohirrim's defense of the Hornburg, though both occurred at the same time.
