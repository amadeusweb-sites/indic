# Battle of Pelargir

*The final fighting of the Kin-strife · 1447 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Battle of Pelargir was the closing phase of the Kin-strife. After Castamir was killed at the Crossings of Erui, his sons and remaining supporters withdrew to the southern port. Eldacar's forces defeated the rebels there, but many escaped by sea to Umbar and continued to threaten Gondor.

## Causes and Context

The Kin-strife began when Castamir displaced Eldacar and took the throne of Gondor. Eldacar later returned from exile and defeated Castamir at the Crossings of Erui, where Castamir was killed. His sons and supporters retreated to Pelargir, using the port and fleet as a final refuge.

The resistance at Pelargir was the last organized phase of the rebellion within Gondor. The battle ended the immediate struggle for the throne, but the escape of the rebels carried the consequences beyond Gondor's borders.

---

## Course of the Battle

1. **The rebels withdraw south.** After Castamir's death at Erui, his sons and many of his followers retreated to Pelargir.
2. **Eldacar pursues the resistance.** His forces attacked the remaining rebels at the port.
3. **The rebels escape by sea.** Many of Castamir's supporters fled to Umbar rather than submit to Eldacar.
4. **The Kin-strife ends in Gondor.** Eldacar secured the throne, but the exiles and their descendants remained enemies of Gondor.

## Outcome and Significance

Eldacar restored the legitimate line of the kings, but the rebellion's divisions had lasting consequences. The exiles established themselves at Umbar, and their descendants became a persistent maritime enemy of Gondor. The battle therefore ended the Kin-strife within the kingdom while carrying its conflict into later wars against the Corsairs.

Castamir was killed at Erui, not at Pelargir. This 1447 event is also distinct from Aragorn's 3019 capture of the Corsair fleet at [Pelargir](./pelargir--corsairs.md).
