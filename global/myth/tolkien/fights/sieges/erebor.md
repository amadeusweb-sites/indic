# Siege of Erebor

*The defense of the Lonely Mountain · 17–27 March 3019*

---

## Overview

After Easterling forces defeated the defenders of Dale, the Men who survived took refuge with the Dwarves inside Erebor. The enemy surrounded the Mountain, cutting off the defenders during the War of the Ring. The siege ended only after news reached the North that Sauron had been defeated.

## Causes and Context

Sauron's northern campaign was coordinated with the assault on Gondor. The Easterlings attacked Dale and Erebor to prevent the Men and Dwarves from aiding the West. After the [Battle of Dale](../battles/dale.md), Brand and Dáin Ironfoot had fallen and their people withdrew into the Mountain.

Erebor was a defensible refuge, but the survivors were cut off from the larger war. The siege is distinct from the initial battle outside the Mountain and from the fighting in Dale.

---

## Course of the Siege

1. **The defenders retreat into Erebor.** After the fall of Dale and the deaths of Brand and Dáin, the survivors took shelter behind the Mountain's gates.
2. **The Easterlings surround the Mountain.** The enemy besieged the combined Dwarven and human force.
3. **The defenders hold out.** Thorin Stonehelm and Bard II led their peoples through the siege, though they could not know the outcome of the war in the south.
4. **Sauron's defeat reaches the North.** The destruction of the One Ring removed the purpose and command behind the attack.
5. **The besieged break out.** The defenders sallied forth and defeated the Easterlings, ending the siege.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The siege ended in a victory for the defenders, but it followed the deaths of both allied kings and the loss of Dale. The survival of Erebor preserved the Dwarven kingdom and its alliance with Dale. Thorin Stonehelm succeeded Dáin, and Bard II succeeded Brand.

The siege's end was connected to the destruction of the One Ring far to the south. The outcome shows that the War of the Ring's conclusion changed battles beyond Mordor itself. The companion [Battle of Erebor](../battles/erebor.md) covers the initial defense and the deaths of the kings.
