# Siege of Minas Ithil

*Sauron's capture of Isildur's city · 3429 of the Second Age*

---

## Overview

The Siege of Minas Ithil was Sauron's attack on the Gondorian city guarding the western entrance to Mordor. The assault opened the War of the Last Alliance and threatened the realm founded by Isildur and Anárion. Minas Ithil fell, but Isildur escaped north with his family and a seedling of the White Tree.

## Causes and Context

Minas Ithil had been founded by Isildur after the Downfall of Númenor to guard the pass from Mordor into Gondor. Sauron returned to Mordor and attacked the city, opening a direct war against the Númenórean realms in Middle-earth.

The assault threatened both branches of Gondor's royal house. Isildur fled north, while Anárion continued the defense from the western side of the Anduin. Elendil and Gil-galad later formed the Last Alliance and marched against Sauron.

---

## Course of the Siege

1. **Sauron attacks Minas Ithil.** His forces struck Gondor's eastern stronghold and began the war against Isildur's realm.
2. **The city is captured.** Minas Ithil fell to Sauron, and the route into Mordor was no longer guarded by Gondor.
3. **Isildur escapes by sea.** He fled north with his family and a seedling of the White Tree, preserving the northern line of the kings.
4. **Anárion holds the West.** He continued the defense of Gondor from the western side of the Anduin while Sauron advanced.
5. **The Last Alliance is formed.** Elendil and Gil-galad united their forces to oppose Sauron; the later siege of Barad-dûr ended the war.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The fall of Minas Ithil opened the War of the Last Alliance and forced Gondor to fight for survival. Isildur's escape preserved the royal line and the White Tree. The captured city remained under Sauron's control and was later renamed Minas Morgul by the Nazgûl.

This Second Age siege is distinct from the [Sack of Minas Ithil](../sacks/minas-ithil.md) in 2002 of the Third Age, when the Nazgûl captured the city again after Gondor had recovered it.
