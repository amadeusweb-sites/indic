# Siege of Fornost

*The fall of Arthedain's capital · 1974 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Siege of Fornost ended with Angmar's capture of the capital of Arthedain, the last surviving northern kingdom of the Dúnedain. The Witch-king's forces had already broken the other successor realms of Arnor. Fornost fell in 1974; the allied victory at the Battle of Fornost came the following year and destroyed Angmar's power.

## Causes and Context

The Witch-king of Angmar had fought Arnor's successor kingdoms for centuries. By 1974, Arthedain was the last of those kingdoms still standing. Fornost was its capital and principal stronghold; its fall ended the remaining organized rule of the Dúnedain in the North.

Arvedui escaped the city and sought refuge among the Lossoth, but was later lost at sea. Gondor sent an army north in 1975, joining surviving Dúnedain and Elves from Lindon. Their victory at the [Battle of Fornost](../battles/fornost.md) followed the siege and did not restore Arthedain.

---

## Course of the Siege

1. **Angmar attacks the last northern kingdom.** The Witch-king's forces advanced on Fornost after the destruction of Cardolan and Rhudaur.
2. **The capital is besieged.** Arthedain's defenders fought to preserve the city and the last remnant of Arnor's northern realm.
3. **Fornost falls.** The Witch-king captured the city, and Arvedui fled north.
4. **The king is lost.** Arvedui sought aid from the Lossoth but later died when his ship was lost in the ice.
5. **Gondor intervenes.** Eärnur's army arrived in Eriador in 1975 and joined the surviving Dúnedain and Elves of Lindon against Angmar.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The fall of Fornost ended Arthedain, but did not end the Witch-king's power. The allied counterattack in 1975 destroyed Angmar and forced the Witch-king to flee. The distinction between the 1974 siege and the 1975 battle is important: the latter avenged the fall but could not restore the northern kingdom.

The Dúnedain survived as a scattered people, and their line continued through Arvedui's heirs. The later prophecy concerning the Witch-king's death was fulfilled at the [Pelennor Fields](../battles/pelennor-fields.md).
