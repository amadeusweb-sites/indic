# Siege of Ost-in-Edhil

*The fall of Eregion's capital · 1697 of the Second Age*

---

## Overview

The Siege of Ost-in-Edhil was part of Sauron's invasion of Eregion during the War of the Elves and Sauron. Sauron sought the Rings of Power and attacked the realm of Celebrimbor after the Elves refused to yield them. The city fell, Celebrimbor was captured and killed, and Elrond withdrew with survivors to establish Imladris.

## Causes and Context

Ost-in-Edhil was the center of the Gwaith-i-Mírdain, whose craft had produced the Rings of Power. Sauron's invasion sought not only territory but control of the Rings. The Three had already been sent away, but the city and its people remained exposed.

The siege was one phase of the [Sack of Ost-in-Edhil](../sacks/ost-in-edhil.md) and the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../wars/elves-and-sauron.md). This page focuses on the city's defense and capture; the sack entry covers the plundering and aftermath.

---

## Course of the Siege

1. **Sauron invades Eregion.** His forces defeated the Elves in the field and advanced on Ost-in-Edhil.
2. **The city is invested.** Celebrimbor and the remaining defenders resisted while Sauron sought the Rings and the knowledge of their making.
3. **Ost-in-Edhil falls.** Sauron's army entered the city and captured Celebrimbor.
4. **Celebrimbor is killed.** He refused to reveal the location of the Three Rings and was slain by Sauron.
5. **Elrond withdraws north.** He escaped with a remnant of the Elves and established Imladris as a refuge.
6. **The war continues westward.** Sauron advanced across Eriador, but his army was later defeated near the Gwathló.

## Outcome and Significance

The fall of Ost-in-Edhil ended Eregion as an independent realm and destroyed its main center of craft. Sauron recovered the Nine Rings made for Men, but not the Three, which had been sent away. Celebrimbor's death deprived the Elves of their chief ring-maker.

Elrond's retreat preserved a refuge and military force in the north. Sauron's victory was not final: Númenórean aid later turned the campaign at the [Battle of the Gwathló](../battles/gwathlo.md).
