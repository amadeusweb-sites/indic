# First Kinslaying at Alqualondë

*The Noldor attack the Teleri · 1495 of the Years of the Trees*

---

## Overview

The First Kinslaying was the battle at Alqualondë in which Noldor attacked the Teleri to seize the ships needed for their return to Middle-earth. The Teleri refused to give up the vessels they had built and kept in their haven. The conflict became the first recorded killing of Elf by Elf and stained the Noldor's departure from Aman with the blood of their kin.

The attack began with Fëanor's followers. Fingolfin's host arrived during the fighting and, believing their kin were under attack, joined in; their intervention helped turn the battle against the Teleri. The Noldor took the ships, but the victory brought no reconciliation. The Doom of Mandos followed, and Fëanor's later burning of the ships at Losgar was a separate act that left Fingolfin's people to cross the Helcaraxë.

## Causes and Context

After Morgoth stole the Silmarils and killed Finwë, Fëanor urged the Noldor to leave Aman and pursue him. He and his sons had sworn an oath to recover the jewels from anyone who withheld them. The Teleri, however, had taken no part in the rebellion and would not lend or surrender the ships they had made. Their refusal was grounded in their attachment to the ships and their loyalty to the Valar, not support for Morgoth.

Fëanor's host reached Alqualondë and demanded passage. When the Teleri rejected the demand, the Noldor tried to take the ships by force. This was not a battle between two armies already at war: it was an attack by one people upon another kindred people who had refused to assist their departure.

---

## Course of the Kinslaying

1. **The Teleri refuse the ships.** Fëanor asked Olwë and the Teleri to provide vessels for the crossing to Middle-earth. Olwë refused, explaining that the ships were the work and possession of his people and that they would not join the Noldor's rebellion.

2. **Fëanor's followers attack.** The Noldor moved to seize the ships. The Teleri resisted in the harbor and initially drove the attackers back. The fighting spread among the ships and quays, where the Teleri's knowledge of the haven and their resistance mattered.

3. **Fingolfin's host joins the fighting.** The host following Fingolfin arrived while the battle was underway. Seeing their kin engaged, many Noldor assumed the Teleri had attacked first and entered the battle on Fëanor's side. Their arrival shifted the balance against the Teleri.

4. **The Noldor take the fleet.** The Teleri were overcome, and many were killed. The surviving Noldor seized the ships and continued north along the coast. The act became known as the First Kinslaying.

5. **The departure brings further division.** At the edge of Aman, the Noldor heard the Doom of Mandos, which foretold exile and grief for their rebellion and bloodshed. Fëanor later took the ships with his own followers and burned them at Losgar. Fingolfin and those left behind crossed the Helcaraxë instead; that abandonment was a later betrayal, distinct from the battle at Alqualondë.

## Outcome and Significance

The Noldor obtained the fleet, but the attack made their departure from Aman an act of violence against kindred. The Doom of Mandos marked the consequence of that choice, and the burning at Losgar later divided Fëanor's followers from Fingolfin's host. Together, the Kinslaying and the abandonment shaped the Noldor's return to Middle-earth.

The assault at Alqualondë was the first of three Kinslayings. The later attacks at [Doriath](./doriath--second.md) and the [Havens of Sirion](./havens-of-sirion--third.md) repeated the attempt to claim the Silmarils by force, but involved different leaders, victims, and circumstances.

## Editorial Note

The account distinguishes Fëanor's initial attack from Fingolfin's followers joining later in confusion. It also distinguishes the Kinslaying from the burning of the ships at Losgar: both shaped the Flight of the Noldor, but they were separate events.
