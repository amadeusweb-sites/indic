# Second Kinslaying at Doriath

*The sons of Fëanor attack Dior · 506 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Second Kinslaying was the assault on Doriath by the sons of Fëanor, who sought the Silmaril held by Dior, son of Beren and Lúthien and heir of Thingol. The jewel had come to Doriath with Beren and Lúthien; after their deaths, Dior wore it openly. The demand for the Silmaril led to a battle among Elves who had once been allies, and to the destruction of much of Doriath's remaining leadership.

The attack took place in 506 of the First Age, after Thingol's death and Melian's departure had left Doriath without the protection of the Girdle of Melian. Dior did not answer the demand sent by the Fëanorians. Their assault killed Dior and Nimloth, while their own side lost Celegorm, Curufin, and Caranthir. Dior's daughter Elwing escaped with the Silmaril, carrying the conflict forward to the Havens of Sirion.

## Causes and Context

The Silmaril's path to Doriath began with Beren and Lúthien's quest. After their return to Middle-earth, the jewel passed to their son Dior. The sons of Fëanor believed their oath required them to reclaim it, though it had been won from Morgoth at great cost and was held by their own kin.

Doriath was vulnerable by the time the demand arrived. Thingol had been killed after a dispute with the Dwarves over the Nauglamír, and Melian left Middle-earth in grief, ending the protection of her Girdle. The sons of Fëanor sent word to Dior demanding the Silmaril. Dior sent no answer; the demand was followed by an armed attack.

---

## Course of the Kinslaying

1. **Dior inherits the Silmaril.** After Beren and Lúthien's deaths, Dior came to Menegroth and took up his inheritance. He wore the Silmaril, making its presence in Doriath known to the sons of Fëanor.

2. **The Fëanorians demand the jewel.** The sons of Fëanor sent Dior a message claiming the Silmaril under their oath. Dior did not reply. The text does not describe a negotiation or a battle plan from his side before the assault.

3. **Doriath is attacked.** The sons of Fëanor came against Menegroth and fighting broke out. Dior and his wife Nimloth were slain. Celegorm, Curufin, and Caranthir were killed by Dior's people; Maedhros and Maglor were the only sons of Fëanor left alive after the battle.

4. **Dior's sons disappear.** Eluréd and Elurín were captured by servants of Celegorm and abandoned in the forest. Maedhros later regretted this cruelty and searched for them, but they were never found. Their fate is left unresolved in the published account.

5. **Elwing escapes with the jewel.** Dior and Nimloth's daughter Elwing escaped with the Silmaril to the Havens of Sirion. The Fëanorians did not recover the jewel, and the survivors from Doriath became part of the community that would later be attacked there.

## Outcome and Significance

The Second Kinslaying devastated Doriath and ended the rule of Thingol's house. Elwing's escape kept the Silmaril out of the hands of the sons of Fëanor, while the loss of Eluréd and Elurín left Dior's sons' fate unknown. The battle also cost the Fëanorians three of their own leaders: Celegorm, Curufin, and Caranthir.

The attack did not settle the oath. Elwing carried the jewel to the Havens of Sirion, where refugees from Doriath lived with other survivors of Beleriand. The sons of Fëanor would pursue her there in the [Third Kinslaying](./havens-of-sirion--third.md). This event also overlaps with the [Sack of Doriath](../sacks/doriath.md): the pages describe the same assault from the perspectives of the kinslaying and the fall of the realm.
