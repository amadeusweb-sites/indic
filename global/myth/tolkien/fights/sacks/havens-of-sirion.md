# Sack of the Havens of Sirion

*The attack on the last refuge of the Silmaril · 538 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Sack of the Havens of Sirion was the final attack by the sons of Fëanor to recover the Silmaril. Elwing and the survivors of Doriath had made a refuge at the mouths of Sirion, joined by people who had escaped other fallen realms. The assault became the Third Kinslaying and scattered the last community of exiles in Beleriand.

## Causes and Context

The Havens of Sirion had become a refuge for people displaced by the fall of Nargothrond, Gondolin, and Doriath. Elwing carried the Silmaril recovered by Beren and Lúthien. The sons of Fëanor invoked their oath and demanded the jewel, making the settlement their final target.

The attack was not only a struggle over territory or treasure. It destroyed a community made up of survivors from several fallen realms and divided the remaining sons of Fëanor from other Elves.

---

## Course of the Sack

1. **The demand is made.** The sons of Fëanor demanded that Elwing surrender the Silmaril.
2. **The Havens are attacked.** When Elwing refused, the attackers assaulted the settlement. The fighting became the Third Kinslaying.
3. **The settlement is destroyed.** Many of the inhabitants were killed or scattered, and the refuge ceased to function as a community.
4. **Elwing escapes with the Silmaril.** She cast herself into the sea; Ulmo bore her away in the form of a white bird.
5. **Elrond and Elros are taken into the attackers' care.** Maedhros and Maglor found the children and kept them, with Maglor treating them with compassion.
6. **Eärendil's voyage continues.** Elwing was reunited with Eärendil, and together they carried the Silmaril west, leading to the Valar's intervention.

## Outcome and Significance

The attack destroyed the last major refuge of the exiles in Beleriand and became the Third Kinslaying. The Silmaril escaped the attackers, and Elwing's reunion with Eärendil made possible the voyage that brought the Valar into the War of Wrath.

The fate of Elrond and Elros is a separate aftermath from the destruction of the Havens. This event is also catalogued as the [Third Kinslaying](../kinslayings/havens-of-sirion--third.md).
