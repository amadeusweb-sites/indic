# Sack of Menegroth

*The Dwarves of Nogrod attack Doriath · 503 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Sack of Menegroth was carried out by the Dwarves of Nogrod after Thingol commissioned them to set the Silmaril in the Nauglamír. A quarrel over the jewel led to Thingol's murder and the Dwarves' seizure of the treasury. Melian left Middle-earth, and the protection of Doriath was broken.

## Causes and Context

Thingol had commissioned the Dwarves of Nogrod to set the Silmaril in the Nauglamír. When the work was complete, a dispute arose over ownership: the Dwarves claimed the necklace as their craft, while Thingol regarded it as his treasure. The argument turned violent, and Thingol was killed.

The Dwarves then plundered Menegroth and escaped with the necklace and Silmaril. The sack followed the weakening of Doriath and preceded the later attack on Dior, described in the [Sack of Doriath](./doriath.md).

---

## Course of the Sack

1. **The Nauglamír is completed.** Dwarven craftsmen set the Silmaril in the necklace at Thingol's request.
2. **The dispute turns deadly.** Thingol refused to yield the treasure; the Dwarves killed him.
3. **Menegroth is plundered.** The Dwarves seized the Nauglamír and Silmaril and left the city.
4. **Melian departs.** After Thingol's death, Melian returned to Valinor, and the Girdle that had protected Doriath ceased to guard it.
5. **Beren pursues the Dwarves.** He gathered the Green-elves of Ossiriand and defeated the returning host at [Sarn Athrad](../battles/sarn-athrad.md), recovering the treasure but not restoring Menegroth.

## Outcome and Significance

Menegroth was devastated, Thingol was dead, and Melian's departure ended the protection of Doriath. Although Beren recovered the Silmaril at Sarn Athrad, the kingdom did not return to its former strength. Dior inherited a weakened realm and later became the target of the sons of Fëanor.

The sack is distinct from Beren's later ambush and from the Second Kinslaying. Together, these events describe the ruin of Doriath and the continuing violence driven by the Silmaril.
