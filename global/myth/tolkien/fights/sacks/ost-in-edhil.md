# Sack of Ost-in-Edhil

*The fall of Eregion's capital · 1697 of the Second Age*

---

## Overview

The Sack of Ost-in-Edhil was part of Sauron's invasion of Eregion in pursuit of the Rings of Power. Sauron's army entered the city, captured Celebrimbor, and destroyed the center of the Elven ring-makers. Elrond escaped with survivors and established Imladris as a refuge.

## Causes and Context

Ost-in-Edhil was the center of the Gwaith-i-Mírdain, the Elven smiths who had made the Rings of Power. Sauron's invasion sought not only territory but control of the Rings and the craft behind them. Celebrimbor resisted, and the Three Rings had already been sent away, preventing Sauron from taking them.

The sack followed the siege and defeat of Eregion's forces. The [Siege of Ost-in-Edhil](../sieges/ost-in-edhil.md) covers the city's defense; this page focuses on its capture, plunder, and the loss of its people and craft.

---

## Course of the Sack

1. **Sauron reaches the city.** After defeating Eregion's forces, his army pressed into Ost-in-Edhil.
2. **The city is captured and plundered.** The settlement and the smiths' works were destroyed.
3. **Celebrimbor is taken.** Sauron captured and killed the leader of the Gwaith-i-Mírdain.
4. **The Rings are seized or hidden.** Sauron recovered the Nine Rings made for Men; the Three had already been sent away.
5. **Elrond withdraws with survivors.** He led the remnant north and established Imladris as a refuge during the continuing war.

---

## Outcome and Significance

The sack ended Eregion as an independent realm and destroyed its chief center of craft. Celebrimbor's death deprived the Elves of the master smith who had made the Rings, while the survival of the Three prevented Sauron from gaining control of all the Rings.

Elrond's retreat preserved an Elvish force in the north. Sauron then advanced across Eriador, but his victory was later reversed at the [Battle of the Gwathló](../battles/gwathlo.md). The sack is part of the wider [War of the Elves and Sauron](../wars/elves-and-sauron.md).
