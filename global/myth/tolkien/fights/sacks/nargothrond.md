# Sack of Nargothrond

*Glaurung's destruction of the hidden realm · 495 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Sack of Nargothrond followed the defeat of the city's army at Tumhalad. Glaurung entered the undefended stronghold after the battle, took control of its halls and treasure, and captured many of its surviving people. The sack ended Nargothrond's independent resistance to Morgoth.

## Causes and Context

Nargothrond had abandoned its hidden defense and built a bridge over the Narog, making its strength more visible. When Glaurung led Morgoth's forces into the region, Orodreth's army marched out to fight and was destroyed at the [Battle of Tumhalad](../battles/tumhalad.md). The city was left without an army to defend it.

The sack was not the battle at Tumhalad itself. It was the subsequent capture of the city and the treatment of those who remained there.

---

## Course of the Sack

1. **Glaurung advances after Tumhalad.** With Nargothrond's field army destroyed, the dragon reached the city.
2. **The stronghold is entered and plundered.** Glaurung took possession of Nargothrond and its treasure.
3. **The people are scattered.** Some were slain, others captured, and some escaped. Finduilas was among those taken prisoner.
4. **The captives are marched away.** Finduilas was later killed by Orcs near the Crossings of Teiglin.
5. **Túrin returns.** He came back after the sack and confronted Glaurung, but could not restore the realm or save Finduilas.

## Outcome and Significance

The fall of Nargothrond removed a major Elvish refuge from western Beleriand and left the region more exposed to Morgoth. Glaurung's presence and deception shaped what followed, but defeating the dragon later did not undo the deaths, captivity, or destruction caused by the sack.

The event is central to the tragedy of Túrin and Finduilas. The field army's defeat, the city's capture, and the later death of Finduilas are connected but distinct episodes in the wider story.

## Editorial Note
