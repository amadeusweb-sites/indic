# Sack of Minas Ithil

*The capture and renaming of Isildur's city · 2002 of the Third Age*

---

## Overview

The Sack of Minas Ithil was the capture of Gondor's eastern city by Sauron's forces, led by the Nazgûl. The city had guarded the western approaches to Mordor and had been founded by Isildur. Its capture transformed the stronghold into Minas Morgul and gave Sauron a base against Gondor.

## Causes and Context

Minas Ithil had been founded by Isildur to guard Gondor's eastern frontier and the route from Mordor. It stood opposite Minas Anor across the Anduin, with Osgiliath between the two cities. Sauron's return to Mordor made Minas Ithil a direct target.

The city fell in 2002, long before the War of the Ring. Its loss placed a hostile power on Gondor's eastern border and changed the balance of the realm. The later [Siege of Minas Ithil](../sieges/minas-ithil.md) belongs to the Last Alliance's campaign in the Second Age and is a separate event.

---

## Course of the Sack

1. **Sauron's forces attack.** The Nazgûl led the assault against Minas Ithil.
2. **The city is captured.** Gondor lost the eastern stronghold, and its defenders were overcome.
3. **The city is renamed.** Sauron's servants called it Minas Morgul, the Tower of Sorcery.
4. **The Nazgûl establish their base.** The captured city became a stronghold from which they threatened Gondor.
5. **Gondor holds elsewhere.** Minas Anor and Osgiliath remained in Gondor's hands, but the frontier was permanently altered.

## Outcome and Significance

The capture of Minas Ithil gave Sauron a fortified position close to Gondor and transformed one of Isildur's cities into a center of fear. Its new name, Minas Morgul, reflected its occupation by the Nazgûl. Gondor retained Minas Anor, later called Minas Tirith, but Osgiliath stood between two hostile powers.

The loss should not be confused with the siege of Minas Ithil during the Last Alliance. The Second Age siege ended with Gondor's city captured by Sauron in 3429; this Third Age sack ended with the Nazgûl taking the city in 2002.
