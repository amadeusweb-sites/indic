# Sack of Doriath

*The Second Kinslaying and the fall of Dior · 506 of the First Age*

---

## Overview

The Sack of Doriath followed the death of Thingol and the departure of Melian. Dior, their heir, received the Silmaril from Beren and Lúthien. When Dior refused the sons of Fëanor's demand for the jewel, they attacked Menegroth and destroyed the remaining strength of Doriath.

## Causes and Context

Doriath had already been weakened by the [Sack of Menegroth](./menegroth.md). Thingol was dead, Melian had departed, and the Girdle that had guarded the realm was gone. Dior inherited a kingdom that had not recovered its former security.

Beren and Lúthien had recovered the Silmaril, and Dior received it after their deaths. The sons of Fëanor invoked their oath and demanded the jewel. Dior's refusal led to an attack on the remaining Sindarin refuge, an assault also known as the Second Kinslaying.

---

## Course of the Sack

1. **Dior inherits Doriath.** He received the Silmaril and sought to rule the diminished kingdom.
2. **The sons of Fëanor demand the jewel.** Dior refused to surrender the Silmaril.
3. **The attackers assault Menegroth.** The sons of Fëanor and their followers attacked the Sindar, destroying the remaining strength of Doriath.
4. **Dior and Nimloth are killed.** The royal couple fell in the fighting.
5. **The children are lost or escape.** Eluréd and Elurín disappeared in the forest; Elwing escaped with the Silmaril to the Havens of Sirion.
6. **The exiles gather at the coast.** Elwing's flight connected the sack to the later attack on the [Havens of Sirion](./havens-of-sirion.md).

---

## Outcome and Significance

The sack ended Doriath as a protected kingdom and scattered its remaining people. The loss of Dior and Nimloth left Elwing as the bearer of the Silmaril. Her escape preserved the jewel and brought it to the Havens of Sirion, where the sons of Fëanor attacked again.

The event is both the destruction of a settlement and the Second Kinslaying. It must be distinguished from the Dwarves' earlier sack of Menegroth in 503 and from the Third Kinslaying at Sirion in 538.
