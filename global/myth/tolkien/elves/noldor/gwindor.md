# Gwindor

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Gwindor is a lord of Nargothrond who is captured during the Nirnaeth Arnoediad and forced to labor in Angband. He escapes and returns to Beleriand with Túrin, bringing news of the enemy's strength.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Lord of Nargothrond
- **Family:** Son of Guilin; brother of Gelmir; beloved of Finduilas.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Gwindor's return changes the course of Nargothrond's politics. He loves Finduilas but sees her drawn to Túrin; wounded and diminished, he dies in the fall of the kingdom after trying to save her.
