# Glorfindel

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Glorfindel is a prominent lord of Gondolin. During the city's escape, he fights a Balrog at the pass of Cirith Thoronath so that Tuor's company can get away.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Lord of the Golden Flower
- **Family:** A lord of Gondolin; leader of the House of the Golden Flower.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Glorfindel and the Balrog both fall from the mountain path. His sacrifice saves many refugees, and his name is later borne by a returned Elf-lord in the Third Age.
