# Egalmoth

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Egalmoth is named among the Noldorin lords of Gondolin. His individual story is brief in the published account, which focuses on the city's fall rather than on the full histories of its houses.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Lord of Gondolin
- **Family:** A lord of Gondolin; the published narrative gives little family detail.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His inclusion among Gondolin's lords records the breadth of the city's leadership and the loss of its people during the attack by Morgoth's forces.
