# Galdor

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Galdor is named among the lords of Gondolin who take part in the city's defense. His identity should be distinguished from other Tolkien characters who share the name.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Galdor of Gondolin
- **Family:** A lord of Gondolin; the published account gives little family detail.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The published Silmarillion mentions Galdor in the story of Gondolin, but provides little individual biography beyond his place among the city's defenders.
