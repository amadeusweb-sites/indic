# Guilin

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Guilin is named in the account of the Nirnaeth Arnoediad through his sons, the Noldorin brothers Gwindor and Gelmir. The published narrative gives little independent biography for him.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Noldorin father of Gwindor and Gelmir
- **Family:** Father of the Noldorin brothers Gwindor and Gelmir.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His name connects the personal tragedy of Gelmir's capture and Gwindor's response to the wider military disaster. The family relationship is part of the emotional context of the battle.
