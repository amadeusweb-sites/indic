# Curufin

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Curufin inherits his father's name and much of his skill in craft. He rules in Himlad alongside Celegorm and later joins him in Nargothrond.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Curufinwë
- **Family:** Fifth son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; father of Celebrimbor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Curufin's ambition and political maneuvering help alienate the people of Nargothrond from the sons of Fëanor. His son Celebrimbor rejects his deeds and remains in the city after Curufin is expelled.
