# Amrod

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Amrod is one of the twin sons of Fëanor. He travels with the Noldor into exile and shares the eastern lands of Beleriand with his brothers.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** One of the twins
- **Family:** Youngest son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; twin brother of Amras.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The published account gives Amrod little individual speech or action. His significance lies chiefly in the collective history of the sons of Fëanor and their continuing pursuit of the Silmarils.
