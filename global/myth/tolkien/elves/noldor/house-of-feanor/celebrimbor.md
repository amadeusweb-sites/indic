# Celebrimbor

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Celebrimbor is a master-smith of the Noldor and the last prominent member of Fëanor's line in Middle-earth. He rejects his father's conduct in Nargothrond and later becomes the leading craftsman of Eregion.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Celebrimbor of Eregion
- **Family:** Son of Curufin and grandson of Fëanor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

In the Second Age he works with the smiths of Eregion and is deceived by Sauron, who helps create the Rings of Power. Celebrimbor's story links the First Age legacy of the Silmarils to the central conflict of the later ages.
