# Amras

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Amras is the twin brother of Amrod and the youngest of Fëanor's sons. Like his brothers, he leaves Aman under the oath and establishes himself in the east of Beleriand.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** One of the twins
- **Family:** Youngest son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; twin brother of Amrod.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The published narrative treats the twins largely together. Amras's place in the legendarium is as a member of Fëanor's house whose fate is bound to the oath and the losses it causes.
