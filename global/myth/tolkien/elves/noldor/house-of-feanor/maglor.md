# Maglor

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Maglor is a renowned singer and poet as well as a son of Fëanor. He rules in eastern Beleriand with Maedhros and remains one of the few members of his house to survive the wars against Morgoth.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Makalaurë; Kanafinwë
- **Family:** Second son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; brother of Maedhros and the other sons of Fëanor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Maglor's history is inseparable from the oath sworn by the sons of Fëanor. In the final attempt to seize the Silmarils, he takes one but is unable to keep it; the text leaves him wandering beside the sea in grief.
