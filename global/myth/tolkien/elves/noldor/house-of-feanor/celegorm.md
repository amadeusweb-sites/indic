# Celegorm

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Celegorm is a skilled hunter and friend of Oromë in Valinor. In Middle-earth he rules in Himlad with his brother Curufin and becomes closely associated with the hound Huan.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Celegorm the Fair
- **Family:** Third son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; brother of Maedhros, Maglor, Caranthir, Curufin, Amrod, and Amras.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His actions in Nargothrond and his attempt to claim Lúthien for himself contribute to the collapse of his political standing. He remains bound to the oath of Fëanor until his death in the attack on Doriath.
