# Nerdanel

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Nerdanel is a Noldorin sculptor and craftswoman whose independent judgment distinguishes her from Fëanor. She is remembered for making lifelike figures in stone and metal and for understanding her husband's difficult temperament.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Nerdanel the Wise
- **Family:** Wife of Fëanor and mother of his seven sons; daughter of the smith Mahtan.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Nerdanel's relationship with Fëanor becomes strained as his pride and anger grow. She does not accompany him into exile, leaving the sons of Fëanor to follow their father's oath without her.
