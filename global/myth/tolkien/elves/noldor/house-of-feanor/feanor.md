# Fëanor

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Fëanor is the greatest craftworker among the Noldor and the maker of the Silmarils, jewels that preserve the light of the Two Trees. His brilliance is matched by a fierce independence and a refusal to yield what he considers his own.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Fëanáro; Curufinwë
- **Family:** Son of Finwë and Míriel; husband of Nerdanel; father of Maedhros, Maglor, Celegorm, Caranthir, Curufin, Amrod, and Amras.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

After Morgoth steals the Silmarils and kills Finwë, Fëanor swears a binding oath with his sons. Their pursuit of the jewels brings the Noldor into exile and repeatedly turns kin against kin.
