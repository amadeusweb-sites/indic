# Maedhros

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Maedhros is the eldest son of Fëanor. Captured by Morgoth after the Dagor-nuin-Giliath, he is chained to Thangorodrim and rescued by Fingon, an act that begins a lasting friendship between the houses of Fëanor and Fingolfin.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Maitimo; Russandol
- **Family:** Eldest son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; brother of Maglor, Celegorm, Caranthir, Curufin, Amrod, and Amras.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Maedhros tries at times to restrain his brothers and build unity among the Noldor, but remains bound by the oath. His later choices show both the possibility of reconciliation and the destructive force of that vow.
