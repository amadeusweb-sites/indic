# Caranthir

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Caranthir is the stern and quick-tempered son of Fëanor who rules in Thargelion, east of Doriath. His lands border the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains, with whom he establishes trade and contact.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Carnistir; the Dark
- **Family:** Fourth son of Fëanor and Nerdanel; brother of the other sons of Fëanor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Caranthir's dealings with the Dwarves show a practical side to his rule, but suspicion and hostility shape his relations with other peoples. He dies with his brothers during the assault on Doriath.
