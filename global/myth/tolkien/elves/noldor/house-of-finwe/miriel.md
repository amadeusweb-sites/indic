# Míriel Serindë

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Míriel is a Noldorin craftswoman celebrated for her skill in embroidery. After Fëanor's birth, she is overcome by weariness and chooses to leave embodied life, becoming the first of the Eldar to die in Aman.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Serindë, the Broideress
- **Family:** First wife of Finwë and mother of Fëanor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Her departure creates an unprecedented question about marriage and death among the Eldar. Finwë's later marriage to Indis and the divided household of his sons become important background to the Noldorin conflict.
