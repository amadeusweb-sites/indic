# Finwë

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Finwë is the first High King of the Noldor in Aman. His leadership guides his people from Cuiviénen to Valinor, where they become renowned for craft, learning, and the making of things.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** High King of the Noldor
- **Family:** Father of Fëanor, Fingolfin, Finarfin, and their descendants; husband of Míriel and later Indis.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His death at the hands of Morgoth is the immediate cause of Fëanor's oath and the Noldorin departure from Valinor. The political and family consequences of that death shape much of the First Age.
