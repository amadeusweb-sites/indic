# Gelmir

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Gelmir is a Noldorin Elf captured by Morgoth's forces. His blinding and killing before the Nirnaeth Arnoediad are witnessed by the army of Fingon and provoke a desperate charge.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Gelmir of Nargothrond
- **Family:** Son of Guilin and brother of Gwindor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Gelmir's fate is one of the events that intensifies the battle's opening. His brother Gwindor leads the first assault, and the Noldor's early success gives way to catastrophic defeat.
