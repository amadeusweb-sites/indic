# Voronwë

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Voronwë is a mariner sent by Turgon to seek a way to Aman. His ship is lost in a storm, but he survives and returns to Middle-earth, where he meets Tuor.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Voronwë of Gondolin
- **Family:** A mariner of Gondolin; son of Aranwë in later accounts.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

As Tuor's guide, Voronwë leads him toward Gondolin and helps deliver Ulmo's message to Turgon. His survival makes him a crucial link between the hidden city and the events beyond it.
