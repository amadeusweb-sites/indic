# Edrahil

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Edrahil is one of the Noldor who accompanies Finrod on the quest to aid Beren. He and the other companions disguise themselves as Orcs, but Sauron discovers them and imprisons them.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elf-lord of Nargothrond
- **Family:** A lord of Nargothrond and a faithful companion of Finrod.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Edrahil dies in Sauron's dungeon. His death precedes Finrod's final sacrifice and underscores the cost of the oath Finrod made to Beren.
