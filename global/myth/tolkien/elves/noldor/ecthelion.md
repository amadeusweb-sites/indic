# Ecthelion

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Ecthelion is one of the foremost warriors of Gondolin. During the city's fall he confronts Gothmog, Lord of Balrogs, at the Fountain Square.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Ecthelion of the Fountain
- **Family:** A lord of Gondolin and captain of the House of the Fountain.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Ecthelion kills Gothmog but is mortally wounded in the struggle. His stand becomes one of the defining acts of resistance during the destruction of Gondolin.
