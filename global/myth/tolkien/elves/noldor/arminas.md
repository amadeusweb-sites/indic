# Arminas

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Arminas is one of the Noldorin messengers who reaches Turgon with a warning from Ulmo. He urges the king of Gondolin to prepare for danger and to consider opening a way to the sea.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Noldorin messenger
- **Family:** A Noldorin Elf associated with the people of Angrod and Aegnor; companion of Gelmir in the message to Turgon.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His warning connects the hidden city of Gondolin with the wider struggle of Beleriand. The message is not acted upon in time to prevent the city's eventual betrayal and fall.
