# Rúmil

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Rúmil is a learned Elf of Tirion associated with the preservation of lore and the invention of a writing system. The published Silmarillion presents him chiefly as a scholar rather than as an actor in the wars of Beleriand.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Rúmil of Tirion
- **Family:** A loremaster of the Noldor in Tirion.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His name represents the Noldorin tradition of record-keeping and linguistic scholarship. References to Rúmil also frame the transmission of early histories about the Valar and the Eldar.
