# Finduilas

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Finduilas is a princess of Nargothrond. Gwindor loves her, and she in turn cares for him, but her life is changed when Túrin arrives and becomes a celebrated leader in the city.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Finduilas of Nargothrond
- **Family:** Daughter of Orodreth and sister of Gil-galad in some later genealogies; the published Silmarillion identifies her as Orodreth's daughter.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Finduilas recognizes the danger of Túrin's influence and tries to persuade him to abandon the war against Morgoth. During the fall of Nargothrond she is captured by Orcs and killed before Túrin can rescue her.
