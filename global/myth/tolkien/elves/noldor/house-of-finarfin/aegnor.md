# Aegnor

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Aegnor is a Noldorin prince who rules in Dorthonion with Angrod. In later accounts of the family, he is remembered for his unfulfilled love for the mortal Andreth, though that story is developed outside the published Silmarillion.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Aikanáro
- **Family:** Son of Finarfin and Eärwen; brother of Finrod, Angrod, Orodreth, and Galadriel.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Aegnor refuses to marry during the war and remains in Middle-earth. He is killed with Angrod in the sudden assault of the Dagor Bragollach.
