# Angrod

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Angrod is a prince of the Noldor who settles in the northern highlands of Dorthonion with his brother Aegnor. He serves as one of the Noldorin leaders guarding the approaches to Dorthonion.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Angaráto
- **Family:** Son of Finarfin and Eärwen; brother of Finrod, Aegnor, Orodreth, and Galadriel.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Angrod is killed with Aegnor during the Dagor Bragollach. His death marks the destruction of the Noldorin defenses in that region and deepens the losses suffered by Finarfin's house.
