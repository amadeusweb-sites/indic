# Galadriel

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Galadriel is one of the leaders of the Noldor who leave Aman, though she takes no part in the Kinslaying at Alqualondë. In Middle-earth she dwells for a time in Doriath and later becomes a major figure of the Second and Third Ages.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Artanis; Nerwen
- **Family:** Daughter of Finarfin and Eärwen; sister of Finrod, Angrod, Aegnor, and Orodreth; wife of Celeborn.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The published Silmarillion gives a brief account of her early life and the long ban that follows the rebellion of the Noldor. Her later history is connected with the Rings of Power and the survival of the Eldar in Middle-earth.
