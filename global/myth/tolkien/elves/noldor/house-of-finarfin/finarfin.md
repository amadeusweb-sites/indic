# Finarfin

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Finarfin is the youngest son of Finwë and the Noldorin prince most closely connected to the Teleri through his wife Eärwen. He turns back from the march into exile after the Kinslaying at Alqualondë.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Arafinwë
- **Family:** Son of Finwë and Indis; husband of Eärwen; father of Finrod, Angrod, Aegnor, Orodreth, and Galadriel.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Finarfin returns to Tirion and becomes High King of the Noldor who remain in Aman. His sons and daughter, however, take part in the wars of Beleriand, making his household a bridge between the two branches of the Noldor.
