# Finrod Felagund

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Finrod is a Noldorin prince and the founder of Nargothrond. He is known for generosity, wisdom, and his friendship with the Men of Bëor's house.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Findaráto; Felagund
- **Family:** Eldest son of Finarfin and Eärwen; brother of Angrod, Aegnor, Orodreth, and Galadriel; beloved of Amarië.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Bound by an oath to Beren, Finrod leaves his kingdom to help retrieve a Silmaril from Angband. He dies in the dungeons of Sauron after defending Beren from a werewolf, fulfilling his promise at the cost of his life.
