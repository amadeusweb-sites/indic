# Orodreth

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Orodreth is a prince of the Noldor who rules Nargothrond after Finrod's death. His daughter Finduilas is loved by Gwindor and later becomes central to Túrin's story.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Artaresto
- **Family:** Son of Finarfin and Eärwen; brother of Finrod, Angrod, Aegnor, and Galadriel; father of Finduilas.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Orodreth is reluctant to expose Nargothrond to open war, but Túrin's influence shifts the kingdom toward confrontation. The dragon Glaurung's assault destroys the city and ends Orodreth's rule.
