# Gil-galad

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Gil-galad is the last High King of the Noldor in Middle-earth. After the fall of the great First Age kingdoms, he rules from Lindon and leads the Elves of the West in the Second Age.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Ereinion
- **Family:** The published Silmarillion identifies him as a son of Fingon; Tolkien's later writings revise his parentage.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Gil-galad resists Sauron's rise, forms the Last Alliance with Elendil, and dies in the War of the Last Alliance. The Rings of Power account presents him as one of the principal defenders of the Free Peoples.
