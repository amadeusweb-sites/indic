# Fingolfin

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Fingolfin is the second son of Finwë and the leader of the largest host of Noldor to cross the Helcaraxë. After Fëanor's death, he becomes High King of the Noldor in Middle-earth.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Nolofinwë
- **Family:** Son of Finwë and Indis; father of Fingon, Turgon, and Aredhel.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His long defiance of Morgoth culminates in a single combat outside Angband. Though he wounds Morgoth, he is killed; the courage of that challenge becomes one of the defining memories of the First Age.
