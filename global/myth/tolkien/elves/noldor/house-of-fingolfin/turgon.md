# Turgon

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Turgon is the founder and king of Gondolin, the hidden city built in the valley of Tumladen. Guided by Ulmo, he discovers the site after the Dagor Aglareb and brings his people there in secrecy.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Turukáno; King of Gondolin
- **Family:** Son of Fingolfin and Indis; husband of Elenwë; father of Idril.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Turgon's commitment to keeping Gondolin hidden protects it for centuries but also delays its evacuation when danger grows. The city is ultimately betrayed by Maeglin and destroyed; Turgon dies defending it.
