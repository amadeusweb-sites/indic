# Fingon

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Fingon is a Noldorin prince remembered for rescuing Maedhros from Thangorodrim. His friendship with Maedhros helps heal the division between their houses and makes possible a period of cooperation among the Noldor.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Findekáno
- **Family:** Eldest son of Fingolfin; brother of Turgon and Aredhel; father of Gil-galad in the published Silmarillion's stated genealogy.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

As High King after Fingolfin, Fingon leads the Noldor in the Nirnaeth Arnoediad. The defeat of that battle ends his reign and leaves the Noldor in Beleriand greatly weakened.
