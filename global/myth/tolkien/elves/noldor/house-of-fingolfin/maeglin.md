# Maeglin

*Noldor and Sindarin descent*

---

## Overview

Born Lómion, Maeglin is raised in Nan Elmoth by Eöl and later follows his mother to Gondolin. He becomes a skilled miner and a powerful lord in the hidden city.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Lómion
- **Family:** Son of Aredhel, a Noldo, and Eöl, a Sinda; nephew of Turgon.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Maeglin's desire for Idril is rejected, and his capture by Morgoth exposes the location and defenses of Gondolin. He betrays the city in exchange for promises that are not honored and is killed during its fall.
