# Aredhel

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Aredhel is a Noldorin princess who loves riding and the forests. Leaving Gondolin with permission from Turgon, she is separated from her companions and eventually reaches Nan Elmoth.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Ar-Feiniel; the White Lady of the Noldor
- **Family:** Daughter of Fingolfin and Indis; sister of Fingon and Turgon; mother of Maeglin.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

In Nan Elmoth she marries Eöl and gives birth to Maeglin. Her attempt to return to Gondolin ends in tragedy when Eöl pursues them and casts a poisoned javelin; Aredhel dies from the wound.
