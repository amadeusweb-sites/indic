# Idril Celebrindal

*Noldor*

---

## Overview

Idril is a princess of Gondolin known for foresight and sound judgment. She distrusts Maeglin and secretly prepares a hidden way out of the city before its fall.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Celebrindal; Silver-foot
- **Family:** Daughter of Turgon and Elenwë; wife of Tuor; mother of Eärendil.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

When Gondolin is attacked, Idril leads survivors through the escape route she had arranged. She later sails with Tuor and Eärendil from the Havens of Sirion, joining the family line that leads to Elrond and Elros.
