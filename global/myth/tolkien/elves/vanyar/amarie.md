# Amarië

*Vanyar*

---

## Overview

Amarië remains in Aman when Finrod departs with the Noldor. Finrod does not marry during his life in Middle-earth, and the account connects this choice with his love for her.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Amarië of the Vanyar
- **Family:** A Vanyarin Elf loved by Finrod Felagund.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Though she does not take part in the wars of Beleriand, Amarië is part of Finrod's personal history and helps explain why he remains unmarried in exile.
