# Ingwë

*Vanyar*

---

## Overview

Ingwë is the High King of the Vanyar and is regarded as the highest-ranking among the Elves. He dwells in Aman and does not lead his people into the rebellion and exile of the Noldor.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** High King of the Vanyar
- **Family:** Leader of the Vanyar and one of the ambassadors who first journey to Valinor.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The Vanyar remain loyal to the Valar and later march with the Host of Valinor in the War of Wrath. Ingwë's kingship represents the Eldar who remained in the West.
