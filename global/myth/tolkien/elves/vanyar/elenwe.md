# Elenwë

*Vanyar*

---

## Overview

Elenwë is a Vanyarin Elf who leaves Aman with the Noldor. During the crossing of the Helcaraxë she is lost in the ice, while Turgon and their daughter Idril survive.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elenwë of the Vanyar
- **Family:** Wife of Turgon and mother of Idril.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Her death marks the cost of the Noldor's exile and remains a defining loss for Turgon's family. Her Vanyarin origin is a reminder that the exile included individuals from more than one Elven kindred.
