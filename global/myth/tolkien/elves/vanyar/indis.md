# Indis

*Vanyar*

---

## Overview

Indis is a Vanyarin Elf who marries Finwë after the death of Míriel. Her marriage creates a new branch of Finwë's family and links the Noldor to the Vanyar.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Indis of the Vanyar
- **Family:** A Vanyarin Elf; second wife of Finwë and mother of Fingolfin and Finarfin.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The different inheritances of Fëanor and his half-brothers become part of the political and personal tensions leading to the Flight of the Noldor. Indis herself remains in Aman.
