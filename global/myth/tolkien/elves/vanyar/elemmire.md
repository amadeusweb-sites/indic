# Elemmírë

*Vanyar*

---

## Overview

Elemmírë is remembered as the composer of the Aldudénië, the lament for the destruction of the Two Trees. The published account gives no further personal history.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Composer of the Aldudénië
- **Family:** A Vanyarin Elf and loremaster or poet.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The song preserves the grief of the Elves of Aman and gives a name to the cultural memory of the Darkening of Valinor.
