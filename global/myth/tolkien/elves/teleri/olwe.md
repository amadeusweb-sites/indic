# Olwë

*Teleri*

---

## Overview

Olwë is one of the leaders of the Teleri and becomes their king in Aman after the division of his people. He rules the coastal city of Alqualondë, where the Teleri build their ships.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** King of the Teleri in Aman
- **Family:** Brother of Elwë Thingol; father of Eärwen.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Olwë refuses Fëanor's demand for ships. The ensuing Kinslaying at Alqualondë marks a turning point in the Noldor's rebellion and in the relationship between the Eldar.
