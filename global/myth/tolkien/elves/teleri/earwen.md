# Eärwen

*Teleri*

---

## Overview

Eärwen is a princess of the Teleri of Alqualondë who marries Finarfin, joining the Telerin and Noldorin royal houses. Her sons and daughter become prominent leaders in the wars of Beleriand.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Eärwen of Alqualondë
- **Family:** Daughter of Olwë; wife of Finarfin; mother of Finrod, Angrod, Aegnor, Orodreth, and Galadriel.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Her kinship with Olwë makes the Kinslaying at Alqualondë a family tragedy as well as a political rupture. The published Silmarillion gives little direct action for Eärwen herself.
