# Eluréd

*Moriquendi — Sindar and human descent*

---

## Overview

Eluréd is one of the young sons of Dior and Nimloth. During the attack on Doriath, the servants of Celegorm carry him and his brother into the forest.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elder son of Dior
- **Family:** Son of Dior and Nimloth; brother of Elurín and Elwing.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The brothers are abandoned and never found. Their fate remains unknown, one of the unresolved losses in the story of Doriath's destruction.
