# Thingol

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Thingol is the Sindarin name of Elwë, one of the leaders of the Teleri who remains in Middle-earth after meeting Melian. He rules Doriath from Menegroth and becomes the chief king of the Sindar.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elwë Singollo; Elu Thingol
- **Family:** Brother of Olwë; husband of Melian; father of Lúthien; grandfather of Dior.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Thingol's protection of Doriath and his changing relations with the Noldor shape much of Beleriand's history. His demand that Beren bring a Silmaril leads to the quest of Beren and Lúthien; his own desire for the jewel later brings ruin upon Doriath.
