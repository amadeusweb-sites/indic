# Denethor

*Moriquendi — Nandor*

---

## Overview

Denethor leads a branch of the Nandor westward into Beleriand. His people settle in Ossiriand and become known as the Laiquendi, or Green-elves.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Denethor of the Green-elves
- **Family:** Son of Lenwë in later genealogical accounts; leader of the Green-elves of Ossiriand.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

When Morgoth's forces invade Beleriand, Denethor's people fight in the First Battle. Denethor is slain on Amon Ereb, and the Green-elves thereafter avoid open war.
