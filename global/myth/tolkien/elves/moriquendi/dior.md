# Dior Eluchíl

*Moriquendi — Half-elven descent*

---

## Overview

Dior is the son of Beren and Lúthien and the heir of Thingol. After receiving the Silmaril from his parents, he returns to Doriath and assumes the kingship.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Eluchíl; Thingol's Heir
- **Family:** Son of Beren and Lúthien; husband of Nimloth; father of Eluréd, Elurín, and Elwing.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Dior refuses to surrender the Silmaril to the sons of Fëanor. Their assault destroys Doriath, and Dior is killed; the survival of Elwing carries the jewel onward.
