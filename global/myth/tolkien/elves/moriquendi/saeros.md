# Saeros

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Saeros is a Sindarin Elf of Doriath whose contempt for Túrin leads to a public quarrel. He mocks Túrin and insults his mother and sister.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Counsellor of Thingol
- **Family:** A counsellor at Thingol's court in Doriath.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

When Saeros attacks Túrin, Túrin overpowers him and lets him flee. Saeros falls while attempting to escape through the forest, and Túrin leaves Doriath believing himself responsible for his death.
