# Elrond

*Half-elven*

---

## Overview

Elrond is one of the Half-elven sons of Eärendil and Elwing. At the end of the First Age he is given the choice of kindreds and chooses the fate of the Elves.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elrond Peredhel; Master of Rivendell
- **Family:** Son of Eärendil and Elwing; twin brother of Elros.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Elrond becomes a leader and healer in Middle-earth, founding Rivendell and preserving lore through later ages. He is a keeper of Vilya and an opponent of Sauron.
