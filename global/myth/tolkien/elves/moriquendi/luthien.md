# Lúthien Tinúviel

*Moriquendi — Sindar and Maia descent*

---

## Overview

Lúthien is the daughter of Thingol and the Maia Melian. Her beauty and songs are renowned, but her defining story is her decision to leave Doriath and share Beren's perilous quest.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Tinúviel; Nightingale
- **Family:** Daughter of Thingol and Melian; beloved of Beren; mother of Dior.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Lúthien enters Angband with Beren, overcomes Morgoth's vigilance, and helps recover a Silmaril. After Beren's death she chooses a mortal fate, becoming the foremother of Dior, Elwing, and the Half-elven line.
