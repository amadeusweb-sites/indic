# Lenwë

*Moriquendi — Nandor*

---

## Overview

Lenwë leads a body of Teleri who refuse to cross the Misty Mountains during the journey west. They become known as the Nandor and settle in the lands east of the mountains.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Leader of the Nandor
- **Family:** A leader of the Teleri who turn aside from the Great Journey; father of Denethor in later genealogical accounts.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The published account gives only a brief outline of Lenwë's choice and migration. His people form one of the branches from which the later Green-elves and Silvan Elves descend.
