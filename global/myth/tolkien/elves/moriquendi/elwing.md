# Elwing

*Moriquendi — Half-elven descent*

---

## Overview

Elwing is the surviving daughter of Dior and Nimloth. She escapes the fall of Doriath with the Silmaril and later lives among the refugees at the Mouths of Sirion.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elwing the White
- **Family:** Daughter of Dior and Nimloth; wife of Eärendil; mother of Elrond and Elros.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

When the sons of Fëanor attack the Havens, Elwing leaps into the sea with the Silmaril and is rescued by Ulmo. She joins Eärendil on his voyage to Aman, where their plea leads to the War of Wrath.
