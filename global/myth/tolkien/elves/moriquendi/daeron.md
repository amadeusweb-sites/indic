# Daeron

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Daeron is a gifted musician and lore-keeper in Doriath. He loves Lúthien and becomes jealous of Beren, whose arrival changes her life.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Minstrel of Doriath
- **Family:** Minstrel and loremaster of Thingol's court; he loves Lúthien.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Daeron reveals Lúthien and Beren's meetings to Thingol, setting the events of their quest in motion. After Lúthien leaves Doriath, Daeron also disappears from the story.
