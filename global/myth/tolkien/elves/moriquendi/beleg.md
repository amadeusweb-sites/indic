# Beleg Cúthalion

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Beleg is one of Thingol's greatest hunters and a captain of the Marchwardens. He becomes a loyal friend and companion to Túrin after Túrin leaves Doriath.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Beleg Strongbow
- **Family:** A Marchwarden of Doriath and close friend of Túrin.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Beleg finds Túrin among the outlaws and follows him into danger. After rescuing him from Orcs, Beleg is accidentally killed by Túrin, an episode that deepens the tragedy of Túrin's life.
