# Falathar

*Moriquendi — Falathrim*

---

## Overview

Falathar is named as one of Eärendil's companions on his westward voyage. The published account records his place in the crew but gives no further personal history.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Mariner of the Havens
- **Family:** One of the mariners who sail with Eärendil.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His inclusion identifies the small company that accompanies Eärendil on the mission that brings the Valar's intervention in the War of Wrath.
