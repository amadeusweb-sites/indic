# Aerandir

*Moriquendi — Falathrim*

---

## Overview

Aerandir is named among the mariners who accompany Eärendil on his final voyage. The published Silmarillion offers no additional account of his life.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Mariner of the Havens
- **Family:** One of the mariners who sail with Eärendil.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

His brief appearance belongs to the transition from the wars of Beleriand to the intervention of the Valar and the end of the First Age.
