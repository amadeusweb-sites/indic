# Círdan

*Moriquendi — Telerin*

---

## Overview

Círdan is the Shipwright who leads the Teleri of the Falas. He remains in Middle-earth through the First Age and builds the ships that connect its peoples with the West.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Círdan the Shipwright; Nowë
- **Family:** A Telerin Elf and leader of the Falathrim; kin of Elwë in some traditions.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Círdan shelters fugitives, aids the messengers sent to Gondolin, and survives the ruin of Beleriand. In the Second Age he gives Narya to Gandalf and remains one of the keepers of the havens.
