# Celeborn

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Celeborn is a Sindarin prince of Doriath who marries Galadriel. Together they leave Beleriand and later become leaders among the Elves of Middle-earth.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Celeborn of Doriath
- **Family:** A kinsman of Thingol in the published Silmarillion; husband of Galadriel.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The published Silmarillion gives only a short account of Celeborn's First Age history. In later ages he and Galadriel rule in Lórien and help resist Sauron.
