# Mablung

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Mablung is a Sindarin captain of Doriath, renowned as a hunter and warrior. He serves Thingol and later undertakes dangerous missions beyond the Girdle of Melian.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Mablung of the Heavy Hand
- **Family:** A captain and trusted servant of Thingol; companion of Beleg.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Mablung is one of the few who understands the peril of the Silmaril in Doriath. He is sent to Nargothrond and later returns to find the kingdom destroyed; he dies in the second Kinslaying.
