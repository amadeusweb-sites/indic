# Elurín

*Moriquendi — Sindar and human descent*

---

## Overview

Elurín is the younger son of Dior and Nimloth. He is taken into the forest with Eluréd during the sons of Fëanor's assault on Doriath.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Younger son of Dior
- **Family:** Son of Dior and Nimloth; brother of Eluréd and Elwing.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Neither brother is seen again, and the text does not resolve whether they survived. Their disappearance emphasizes the human cost of the Kinslaying at Doriath.
