# Nimloth

*Moriquendi — Sindar*

---

## Overview

Nimloth is a Sindarin Elf of Doriath who marries Dior, the heir of Thingol. She is the mother of the children who carry the line of Lúthien into later ages.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Nimloth of Doriath
- **Family:** A kinswoman of Celeborn; wife of Dior; mother of Eluréd, Elurín, and Elwing.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

During the attack on Doriath by the sons of Fëanor, Nimloth is slain along with Dior. Her children are taken into the forest, while Elwing escapes with the Silmaril.
