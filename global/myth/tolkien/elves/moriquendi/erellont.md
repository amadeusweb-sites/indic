# Erellont

*Moriquendi — Falathrim*

---

## Overview

Erellont is one of the named mariners who accompany Eärendil. His history is limited to the voyage west in the final chapter of the Quenta Silmarillion.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Mariner of the Havens
- **Family:** One of the mariners who sail with Eärendil.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

The mariners' journey makes Erellont a witness to the last great passage from Middle-earth to Aman in the First Age.
