# Eöl

*Moriquendi — Telerin*

---

## Overview

Eöl is a skilled smith who dwells in Nan Elmoth and avoids the company of the Noldor. He is associated with the Dwarves and makes the black sword Anglachel.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** The Dark Elf
- **Family:** Husband of Aredhel; father of Maeglin; a kinsman of Thingol.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Eöl marries Aredhel and raises Maeglin in isolation. When they flee to Gondolin, Eöl follows and attempts to kill his son; Aredhel is fatally struck, and Eöl is executed by Turgon's judgment.
