# Eärendil

*Half-elven*

---

## Overview

Eärendil is a mariner born in Gondolin and raised among the survivors of its fall. He later lives at the Mouths of Sirion with Elwing and becomes the one who sails west carrying a Silmaril.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Eärendil the Mariner; the Blessed
- **Family:** Son of Tuor and Idril; husband of Elwing; father of Elrond and Elros.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Eärendil reaches Aman and asks the Valar to aid Elves and Men against Morgoth. His voyage brings the War of Wrath; afterward he is set in the heavens with the Silmaril as the Evening Star.
