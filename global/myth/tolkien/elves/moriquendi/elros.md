# Elros

*Half-elven; chooses the fate of Men*

---

## Overview

Elros is one of the Half-elven sons of Eärendil and Elwing. When offered the choice of kindreds, he chooses the fate of Men and becomes the first king of Númenor.

---

## Names and Relationships

- **Other names or titles:** Elros Tar-Minyatur
- **Family:** Son of Eärendil and Elwing; twin brother of Elrond.

---

## Role in the Legendarium

Elros's long life and royal line shape the history of Númenor. His choice contrasts with Elrond's and establishes the distinct destinies of their descendants.
