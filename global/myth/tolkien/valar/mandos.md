# Mandos (Námo)

> Mandos is the keeper of the Houses of the Dead and the Doomsman of the Valar.

*Valar — Lord of the Houses of the Dead*

## Overview

Mandos is the stern Vala of memory and the dead, though he speaks only what is appointed and not what he desires.

## Identity

- **Title:** Námo; Doomsman of the Valar
- **Role:** Death, memory, fate and the waiting halls
- **Spouse / consort:** Vairë
- **Familiar symbols:** Doors of Night; Halls of Mandos
- **Kindred:** Valar

## Names and Etymology

The name **Mandos (Námo)** is used throughout Tolkien's published legends for this figure.
Where an alternative name is known, it is preserved here as a secondary epithet rather than treated as a separate character.

## Character and Domain

In the Ainulindalë and Valaquenta, Mandos (Námo) is defined less as a mortal-style personage than as a divine power: a will, office and creative theme made visible.
The sphere associated with Mandos (Námo) is **Death, memory, fate and the waiting halls**. Tolkien repeatedly presents the Valar through consorts, crafts, music, duties and the shape of their houses.

## Relationship to Middle-earth

The Valar are the Powers of the World who entered Arda after the Music of the Ainur. They oppose Melkor, preserve creation and generally act through delegated powers rather than constant direct rule.
